By: Mizani H.B. No. 928
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to required municipal and county approval to construct a
  data center.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 250, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 250.015 to read as follows:
         Sec. 250.015.  CONSTRUCTION OF DATA CENTER. (a) In this
  section, "data center" means a facility that is used primarily to
  house servers and related equipment and support staff for the
  processing, storage, and distribution of data.
         (b)  A person may not construct a data center within the
  boundaries of a municipality unless the governing body of the
  municipality votes to approve the construction at a public hearing
  held for that purpose.
         (c)  A person may not construct a data center within the
  unincorporated area of a county unless the commissioners court of
  the county votes to approve the construction at a public hearing
  held for that purpose.
         SECTION 2.  Section 250.015, Local Government Code, as added
  by this Act, applies only to the construction of a data center, as
  defined by that section, for which the first application for a
  building permit or other required municipal or county authorization
  to construct the data center is submitted on or after the effective
  date of this Act.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
