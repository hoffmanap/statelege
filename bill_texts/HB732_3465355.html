By: Perez of El Paso H.B. No. 732
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the ineligibility of certain data centers for state and
  local economic development incentives.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
  ARTICLE 1.  TAX ABATEMENT UNDER CHAPTER 312, TAX CODE
         SECTION 1.01.  Subchapter A, Chapter 312, Tax Code, is
  amended by adding Section 312.0022 to read as follows:
         Sec. 312.0022.  PROHIBITION ON TAX ABATEMENT FOR DATA
  CENTER. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Data center" means any facility that primarily
  contains electronic equipment used to process, store, or transmit
  digital information.  The term includes a facility used primarily
  for cryptocurrency mining or other distributed ledger or blockchain
  computation and a facility used primarily for the training or
  operation of an artificial intelligence model.
               (2)  "Data center" does not include:
                     (A)  a facility owned and operated by this state,
  a political subdivision of this state, or an institution of higher
  education as defined by Section 61.003, Education Code;
                     (B)  electronic equipment, or the space
  containing electronic equipment, that supports a manufacturing,
  health care, financial, retail, or other business operation
  conducted by the owner or operator of the equipment at the same
  site, if housing that equipment is not the primary use of the site;
  or
                     (C)  a facility used primarily by a
  telecommunications provider to place tangible personal property
  that is used to deliver telecommunications services.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a
  taxing unit may not enter into a tax abatement agreement under this
  chapter that provides for the exemption from taxation of all or part
  of the value of:
               (1)  real property on which a data center is located or
  is proposed to be located; or
               (2)  tangible personal property that is located or is
  to be located at a data center.
         (c)  The governing body of a municipality or county may not
  designate an area as a reinvestment zone under this chapter if the
  primary purpose of the designation is to enable a taxing unit to
  enter into an agreement prohibited by Subsection (b).
         (d)  A tax abatement agreement entered into in violation of
  this section is void.
  ARTICLE 2.  MUNICIPAL PROGRAMS UNDER CHAPTER 380, LOCAL GOVERNMENT
  CODE
         SECTION 2.01.  Chapter 380, Local Government Code, is
  amended by adding Section 380.005 to read as follows:
         Sec. 380.005.  PROHIBITION ON PROGRAM BENEFITING DATA
  CENTER.  (a)  In this section, "data center" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 312.0022, Tax Code.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a
  municipality may not establish or administer a program under this
  chapter, and may not enter into an agreement under this chapter,
  that provides a loan, grant, rebate, refund, or other payment of
  public money to a person if the loan, grant, rebate, refund, or
  payment is made, computed, or conditioned wholly or partly on:
               (1)  the construction, expansion, operation, or
  location of a data center in the municipality or in the
  extraterritorial jurisdiction of the municipality; or
               (2)  the amount of ad valorem taxes, sales and use
  taxes, or other taxes or fees paid to the municipality by or
  attributable to a data center or the owner or operator of a data
  center.
         (c)  An agreement entered into in violation of this section
  is void.
         (d)  This section does not prohibit a municipality from
  providing a governmental service to a data center on the same terms
  on which the municipality provides that service to similarly
  situated commercial property.
  ARTICLE 3.  COUNTY PROGRAMS UNDER CHAPTER 381, LOCAL GOVERNMENT
  CODE
         SECTION 3.01.  Chapter 381, Local Government Code, is
  amended by adding Section 381.006 to read as follows:
         Sec. 381.006.  PROHIBITION ON PROGRAM BENEFITING DATA
  CENTER.  (a)  In this section, "data center" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 312.0022, Tax Code.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter,
  including Sections 381.004(g) and (h), the commissioners court of a
  county may not develop or administer a program under this chapter,
  and may not enter into an agreement under this chapter, that
  provides a tax abatement, loan, grant, rebate, refund, or other
  payment of public money to a person if the abatement, loan, grant,
  rebate, refund, or payment is made, computed, or conditioned wholly
  or partly on:
               (1)  the construction, expansion, operation, or
  location of a data center in the county; or
               (2)  the amount of ad valorem taxes, sales and use
  taxes, or other taxes or fees paid to the county by or attributable
  to a data center or the owner or operator of a data center.
         (c)  An agreement entered into in violation of this section
  is void.
         (d)  This section does not prohibit a county from providing a
  governmental service to a data center on the same terms on which the
  county provides that service to similarly situated commercial
  property.
  ARTICLE 4. TAX INCREMENT FINANCING UNDER CHAPTER 311, TAX CODE
         SECTION 4.01.  Chapter 311, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Section 311.0102 to read as follows:
         Sec. 311.0102.  PROHIBITION ON USE OF TAX INCREMENT FOR DATA
  CENTER. (a)  In this section, "data center" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 312.0022.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a
  municipality, a county, or the board of directors of a reinvestment
  zone may not:
               (1)  include in a project plan or reinvestment zone
  financing plan a project cost incurred primarily to benefit a data
  center; or
               (2)  use money in the tax increment fund, or enter into
  an agreement to use money in the tax increment fund, to make a loan,
  grant, rebate, refund, or other payment to a person if the loan,
  grant, rebate, refund, or payment is made, computed, or conditioned
  wholly or partly on:
                     (A)  the construction, expansion, operation, or
  location of a data center in the reinvestment zone; or
                     (B)  the amount of ad valorem taxes, sales and use
  taxes, or other taxes or fees paid by or attributable to a data
  center or the owner or operator of a data center.
         (c)  This section does not prohibit the use of money in the
  tax increment fund for a public work or public improvement that
  serves the reinvestment zone generally and is not constructed
  primarily to serve a data center.
         (d)  An agreement entered into in violation of this section
  is void.  A project plan or reinvestment zone financing plan, or an
  amendment to a plan, is void to the extent it violates this section.
  ARTICLE 5.  CONFORMING AMENDMENT TO THE TEXAS JOBS, ENERGY,
  TECHNOLOGY, AND INNOVATION ACT
         SECTION 5.01.  Section 403.602(8), Government Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
               (8)  "Eligible project":
                     (A)  means a project:
                           (i)  to construct or expand a new or existing
  facility that is:
                                 (a)  a manufacturing facility;
                                 (b)  a facility related to the
  provision of utility services, including an electric generation
  facility that is considered to be dispatchable because the
  facility's output can be controlled primarily by forces under human
  control;
                                 (c)  a facility related to the
  development of natural resources; or
                                 (d)  a facility engaged in the
  research, development, or manufacture of high-tech equipment or
  technology; or
                           (ii)  to construct or expand critical
  infrastructure; and
                     (B)  does not include a project to construct or
  expand a new or existing:
                           (i)  nondispatchable electric generation
  facility; [or]
                           (ii)  electric energy storage facility;
  or[.]
                           (iii)  data center, as defined by Section
  312.0022, Tax Code.
  ARTICLE 6. TEXAS ENTERPRISE FUND
         SECTION 6.01.  Section 481.078, Government Code, is amended
  by adding Subsection (e-2) to read as follows:
         (e-2)  A project that consists of or includes the
  construction, expansion, equipping, or operation of a data center,
  as defined by Section 312.0022, Tax Code, is not an eligible project
  for purposes of this section.  Notwithstanding any other provision
  of this section, the governor may not award a grant under this
  section for such a project.
  ARTICLE 7.  TRANSITION; EFFECTIVE DATE
         SECTION 7.01.  (a)  The changes in law made by this Act apply
  only to an agreement entered into, and to a project plan or
  reinvestment zone financing plan adopted or amended, on or after
  the effective date of this Act.  An agreement entered into, or a
  plan adopted or amended, before the effective date of this Act is
  governed by the law in effect on the date the agreement was entered
  into or the plan was adopted or amended, and the former law is
  continued in effect for that purpose.
         (b)  For purposes of this Act, the renewal or extension of an
  agreement on or after the effective date of this Act, or an
  amendment of an agreement on or after that date that increases the
  amount or extends the duration of a benefit provided under the
  agreement, is considered the entering into of a new agreement on the
  date of the renewal, extension, or amendment.
         SECTION 7.02.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
