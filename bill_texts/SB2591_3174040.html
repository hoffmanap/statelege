A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the purposes for which the Texas Water Development
  Board may use money in the economically distressed areas account.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 17.922(a), Water Code, is amended to
  read as follows:
         (a)  The board shall use the economically distressed areas
  account to provide financial assistance to political subdivisions
  for the construction, acquisition, or improvement of water supply
  and sewer services, including providing funds from the account for:
               (1)  the state's participation in federal programs that
  provide assistance solely for projects intended to serve
  economically distressed areas; or
               (2)  costs associated with platting or replatting an
  area in preparation for connecting the area to a public water supply
  or sanitary sewer system if the area to be platted or replatted was
  developed before the model rules were adopted by the board under
  Section 16.343.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
