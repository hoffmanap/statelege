By: Ochoa H.B. No. 680
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to emergency financing and the issuance of obligations by
  certain coastal water districts.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 49.153, Water Code, is amended by
  amending Subsection (e) and adding Subsections (f), (g), (h), and
  (i) to read as follows:
         (e)  Subsection (c) does not apply to:
               (1)  a note issued to and approved by:
                     (A)  the Farmers Home Administration;
                     (B)  the United States Department of Agriculture;
                     (C)  the Texas Water Development Board;
                     (D)  the North American Development Bank; or
                     (E)  a federally chartered instrumentality of the
  United States authorized under 12 U.S.C. Section 2128(f) to provide
  financing for water and waste disposal facilities, provided that
  the district that executes the note is located wholly in a county
  that:
                           (i)  does not contain a municipality that
  has a population of more than 750,000; and
                           (ii)  is not adjacent to a county described
  by Subparagraph (i); or
               (2)  a district described by Section 49.181(h)[.]; or
               (3)  a note issued in accordance with Subsection (f) by
  a district whose territory is located wholly within one or more
  first tier coastal counties, as defined by Section 2210.003,
  Insurance Code.
         (f)  A district whose territory is located wholly within one
  or more first tier coastal counties, as defined by Section
  2210.003, Insurance Code, may authorize the issuance of revenue
  notes before a disaster declaration and may issue the notes in
  accordance with this section if:
               (1)  the proceeds are used solely for:
                     (A)  planning, acquiring, constructing,
  improving, reinforcing, or protecting district water, wastewater,
  drainage, reclamation, or flood control infrastructure that the
  district's board determines is reasonably necessary to mitigate the
  effects of a foreseeable disaster; or
                     (B)  emergency operation, repair, replacement, or
  restoration of district infrastructure or essential services
  damaged or disrupted by a disaster for which a disaster declaration
  has been made;
               (2)  before the issuance of the notes, the district's
  board adopts a resolution that:
                     (A)  identifies the purposes for which the
  proceeds will be used;
                     (B)  specifies the maximum principal amount
  authorized to be issued;
                     (C)  identifies the revenues pledged for
  repayment; and
                     (D)  includes a finding that the district
  reasonably expects the pledged revenues to be sufficient to pay the
  principal of and interest on the notes when due; and
               (3)  the notes comply with the requirements for
  attorney general approval and comptroller registration under
  Subsection (g).
         (g)  Before a note authorized under Subsection (f) is
  delivered to a purchaser, the note and proceedings authorizing its
  issuance must be submitted to the attorney general for examination
  and approval and to the comptroller for registration in the same
  manner as provided by Section 49.184 for bonds. For a program
  authorizing the issuance of commercial paper notes, the approval
  and registration may apply to the program and the notes issued under
  the program to the extent permitted by other applicable law. A note
  issued under Subsection (f) is incontestable only after the
  required approval and registration. For purposes of Subsection
  (f), the term "note" includes a commercial paper note that has a
  stated maturity of 365 days or fewer from the date of issuance and
  meets the credit-rating and, if applicable, credit-support
  requirements of Section 2256.013(2), Government Code.
         (h)  In this section, "disaster declaration" means a
  declaration of a state of disaster or emergency issued by the
  president of the United States under federal law, or a declaration
  of a state of disaster issued under Chapter 418, Government Code,
  that applies to all or part of the territory of the district. The
  term includes an amendment, modification, or extension of the
  declaration.
         (i)  Not later than the 30th day after the date a district
  issues a note under Subsection (f), the district shall provide the
  commission a copy of the resolution adopted under Subsection (f)(2)
  and a written summary identifying the principal amount issued, the
  intended uses of the proceeds, and the pledged source of repayment.
  Submission under this subsection is for notice and does not require
  commission approval of the note.
         SECTION 2.  Section 49.154, Water Code, is amended by
  amending Subsection (d) and adding Subsection (e) to read as
  follows:
         (d)  Except as provided by Subsection (e),
  [Districts]districts required to seek commission approval of bonds
  must have an application for such approval on file with the
  commission prior to the issuance of bond anticipation notes.
         (e)  During a disaster declaration described by Section
  49.153(h), a district whose territory is located wholly within one
  or more first tier coastal counties, as defined by Section
  2210.003, Insurance Code, may issue bond anticipation notes to
  finance costs described by Section 49.153(f)(1)(B) without having
  an application for commission approval of bonds on file as
  otherwise required by Subsection (d). This subsection does not
  affect the requirements of Subsections (a), (b), and (c), including
  the maturity limit applicable to bond anticipation notes and tax
  anticipation notes, or expand the purposes for which tax
  anticipation notes may be issued.
         SECTION 3.  Section 49.181(a), Water Code, is amended by
  amending Subdivision (5) and adding Subdivision (6) to read as
  follows:
               (5)  bonds issued by a public utility agency created
  under Chapter 572, Local Government Code, any of the public
  entities participating in which are districts if at least one of
  those districts is a district described by Subsection (h)(1)(E)[.];
  or
               (6)  bonds issued by a district whose territory is
  located wholly within one or more first tier coastal counties, as
  defined by Section 2210.003, Insurance Code, during a disaster
  declaration described by Section 49.153(h) or not later than the
  180th day after the date the declaration expires, if:
                     (A)  the proceeds are used solely to finance
  emergency operation, repair, replacement, or restoration of
  district infrastructure or essential services damaged or disrupted
  by the disaster;
                     (B)  before the bonds are issued, the district's
  board adopts a resolution identifying the disaster, the projects to
  be financed, the estimated costs, and the source of repayment, and
  finding that the projects are reasonably necessary to restore or
  maintain essential services; and
                     (C)  not later than the 30th day after the bonds
  are issued, the district provides the commission a copy of the
  resolution and a summary of the principal amount issued and the
  intended uses of the proceeds.
         SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution. If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
