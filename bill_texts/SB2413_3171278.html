A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the applicability of certain laws to tiny homes and tiny
  home communities.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 94.002, Property Code, is amended by
  adding Subsection (c) to read as follows:
         (c)  This chapter does not apply to a tiny home community or
  tiny home.  For purposes of this subsection:
               (1)  "Tiny home" means a moveable trailer:
                     (A)  with a home affixed to a single chassis:
                           (i)  which is a transportable unit that has a
  body not wider than 14 feet and is designed to provide living
  quarters for not more than one household when connected to
  utilities necessary for operation of installed fixtures and
  appliances; and
                           (ii)  the total area of which in a setup
  mode, when measured from the maximum dimensions of the exterior
  surface of the exterior stud walls, not including any bay windows or
  protrusions, does not exceed 500 square feet;
                     (B)  that is not:
                           (i)  a recreational vehicle as defined by
  Section 522.004(b), Transportation Code;
                           (ii)  a park model trailer that is designed
  to be used for human habitation, with or without a permanent
  foundation, when connected to the required utilities, and that:
                                 (a)  is less than eight feet six inches
  in width and 45 feet in length in the traveling mode;
                                 (b)  includes the plumbing, heating,
  air conditioning, and electrical systems; and
                                 (c)  is not required to be affixed with
  a label or decal issued by the United States Department of Housing
  and Urban Development and by the Texas Department of Housing and
  Community Affairs; or
                           (iii)  a manufactured home as defined by
  Section 94.001, Property Code; and
                     (C)  that may not be titled as a vehicle with the
  Texas Department of Motor Vehicles.
               (2)  "Tiny home community" means real property that: 
                     (A)  is rented or held out for rent to others for
  the placement of two or more tiny homes;
                     (B)  is not:
                           (i)  a recreational vehicle park as defined
  by Section 13.087, Water Code, and not under ownership of a
  recreational vehicle park; or
                           (ii)  a manufactured home community as
  defined by Section 94.001 and not under ownership of a manufactured
  home community;
                     (C)  is subject to property tax; and
                     (D)  is metered by a utility company to the owners
  of the tiny homes on the property.
         SECTION 2.  Section 522.004(b), Transportation Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         (b)  In this section, "recreational vehicle" means a motor
  vehicle primarily designed as temporary living quarters for
  recreational camping or travel use. The term includes a travel
  trailer, camping trailer, truck camper, and motor home.  The term
  does not include a tiny home as defined by Section 94.002(c),
  Property Code.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
