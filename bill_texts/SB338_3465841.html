A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to data centers.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 403.602, Government Code, is amended by
  adding Subdivision (7-a) to read as follows:
               (7-a)  "Data center" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 312.2042, Tax Code.
         SECTION 2.  Section 403.605(a), Government Code, is amended
  to read as follows:
         (a)  The taxable value for school district maintenance and
  operations ad valorem tax purposes of eligible property subject to
  an agreement for each tax year of the incentive period prescribed by
  the agreement is equal to:
               (1)  50 percent of the market value of the property for
  that tax year; [or]
               (2)  if the property is used as part of an eligible
  project that is a data center, not more than 50 percent of the
  market value of the property for that tax year; or
               (3)  if the property is located in a qualified
  opportunity zone, 25 percent of the market value of the property for
  that tax year.
         SECTION 3.  Section 403.612(d), Government Code, is amended
  to read as follows:
         (d)  This subsection applies to a term described by
  Subsection (b)(10). The agreement must provide that:
               (1)  the governor or the school district is authorized
  to terminate the agreement if the applicant fails to comply with:
                     (A)  an applicable jobs or wage requirement of the
  agreement; or
                     (B)  any term of an agreement that pertains to an
  eligible project that is a data center;
               (2)  the governor or the district may not terminate the
  agreement until the party provides written notice to the applicant
  of the proposed termination;
               (3)  the governor or the district must provide the
  applicant a 180-day period to cure and dispute the alleged failure,
  including through judicial action; and
               (4)  in the event the agreement is terminated, the
  state shall recover from the applicant a penalty in an amount equal
  to all lost ad valorem tax revenue from the project and interest on
  that amount calculated as provided by Section 111.060, Tax Code.
         SECTION 4.  Subchapter T, Chapter 403, Government Code, is
  amended by adding Section 403.6121 to read as follows:
         Sec. 403.6121.  ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR AGREEMENTS
  INVOLVING DATA CENTERS. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Closed-cycle recirculating system," "dry cooling
  system," "saline water cooling system," and "water-efficient
  cooling system" have the meanings assigned by Section 39.920,
  Utilities Code.
               (2)  "Commission" means the Public Utility Commission
  of Texas.
         (b)  This section applies only to an agreement entered into
  under Section 403.612 pertaining to an eligible project that is a
  data center.
         (c)  In addition to the requirements of Section 403.612, an
  agreement entered into under that section between the governor, a
  school district, and an applicant pertaining to an eligible project
  that is a data center must require the applicant to:
               (1)  use only a closed-cycle recirculating system, dry
  cooling system, saline water cooling system, water-efficient
  cooling system, or a combination of such systems to cool the data
  center, including any generation resource located at the data
  center;
               (2)  at the time the applicant executes the agreement,
  submit to the commission documentation in a form prescribed by the
  commission that:
                     (A)  estimates the anticipated annual water
  supply needs for cooling purposes at the data center; and
                     (B)  identifies the anticipated source or sources
  of water the applicant will procure to meet the anticipated annual
  water supply needs of the data center;
               (3)  starting on the first anniversary of the
  construction completion date of the data center and on each
  subsequent anniversary of that date until the data center is no
  longer in operation, submit to the commission documentation in a
  form prescribed by the commission that states for the preceding
  year:
                     (A)  the amount of water used for cooling
  purposes; and
                     (B)  the source or sources of water used for
  cooling purposes;
               (4)  own the data center for the term of the agreement;
               (5)  pay the cost to remediate environmental harm to
  the real property that is the subject of the agreement that results
  from the construction, operation, presence, abandonment, or
  demolition of the data center;
               (6)  except as provided by Subsection (d), convey to
  the school district a nonpossessory interest in the real property
  that is the subject of the agreement that imposes a permanent
  limitation on the applicant and the applicant's assignees or
  successors in interest from constructing or operating a data center
  that uses a cooling system other than a system or combination of
  systems described by Subdivision (1), and file for recording in the
  real property records of the county in which the real property is
  located the instrument of conveyance of the interest; and
               (7)  comply with any standards adopted by the
  commission under Section 39.920, Utilities Code, limiting light and
  noise pollution caused by operation of the data center or
  regulating the minimum distance of any component building of the
  data center to the boundary of the data center.
         (d)  An agreement is not required to provide for the
  conveyance of a nonpossessory interest in real property as provided
  by Subsection (c)(6) if the applicant has conveyed an interest in
  the property to a taxing unit under an agreement entered into under
  Chapter 312, Tax Code.
         SECTION 5.  Chapter 380, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 380.005 to read as follows:
         Sec. 380.005.  ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR PROGRAMS
  INVOLVING DATA CENTERS. (a)  In this section, "data center" has the
  meaning assigned by Section 312.2042, Tax Code.
         (b)  A municipality may not make a loan or grant of public
  money under a program authorized by this chapter involving the
  construction or operation of a data center unless the governing
  body of the municipality or a development corporation created by
  the municipality enters into an agreement with the loan or grant
  recipient that meets the applicable requirements of Section
  312.2042, Tax Code.
         SECTION 6.  Section 381.004(g), Local Government Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         (g)  The commissioners court may develop and administer a
  program authorized by Subsection (b) for entering into a tax
  abatement agreement with an owner or lessee of a property interest
  subject to ad valorem taxation.  The execution, duration, and other
  terms of the agreement are governed, to the extent practicable, by
  the provisions of Sections 312.204, 312.2042, 312.205, and 312.211,
  Tax Code, as if the commissioners court were a governing body of a
  municipality.
         SECTION 7.  Section 312.204, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Subsection (a-1) to read as follows:
         (a-1)  In this subsection, "data center" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 312.2042.  Notwithstanding any other provision
  of this section, the governing body of a municipality may not enter
  into an agreement under this section with the owner or lessee of
  property that is a data center to exempt from taxation more than 50
  percent of the portion of the value of the property that is
  otherwise subject to abatement under this section during any year
  covered by the agreement.
         SECTION 8.  Subchapter B, Chapter 312, Tax Code, is amended
  by adding Section 312.2042 to read as follows:
         Sec. 312.2042.  ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR AGREEMENTS
  INVOLVING DATA CENTERS. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Closed-cycle recirculating system," "dry cooling
  system," "saline water cooling system," and "water-efficient
  cooling system" have the meanings assigned by Section 39.920,
  Utilities Code.
               (2)  "Commission" means the Public Utility Commission
  of Texas.
               (3)  "Data center" means a site containing one or more
  facilities that:
                     (A)  individually or collectively are at least
  100,000 square feet in area; and
                     (B)  are constructed or adapted to house servers
  and related equipment and support staff for the processing,
  storage, and distribution of data.
         (b)  This section applies only to an agreement entered into
  under Section 312.204 pertaining to real and tangible personal
  property located at a data center.
         (c)  In addition to the requirements of Section 312.204, an
  agreement entered into under that section between a municipality
  and the owner or lessee of real property at which a data center is
  located must:
               (1)  require the owner or lessee, as applicable, to:
                     (A)  use only a closed-cycle recirculating
  system, dry cooling system, saline water cooling system,
  water-efficient cooling system, or a combination of such systems to
  cool the data center;
                     (B)  at the time the agreement is executed, submit
  to the commission documentation in a form prescribed by the
  commission that:
                           (i)  estimates the anticipated annual water
  supply needs for cooling purposes at the data center; and
                           (ii)  identifies the anticipated source or
  sources of water the owner or lessee, as applicable, will procure to
  meet the anticipated annual water supply needs of the data center;
                     (C)  starting with the first anniversary of the
  date the data center begins operations and on each subsequent
  anniversary of that date until the data center is no longer in
  operation, submit to the commission documentation in a form
  prescribed by the commission that states for the preceding year:
                           (i)  the amount of water used for cooling
  purposes; and
                           (ii)  the source or sources of water used for
  cooling purposes;
                     (D)  either:
                           (i)  own the data center for the term of the
  agreement if the owner enters the agreement; or
                           (ii)  lease the data center for the term of
  the agreement if the lessee enters the agreement;
                     (E)  pay the cost to remediate environmental harm
  to the real property that is subject to the agreement that results
  from the construction, operation, presence, abandonment, or
  demolition of the data center;
                     (F)  except as provided by Subsection (d), convey
  to the municipality a nonpossessory interest in the real property
  that is the subject of the agreement that imposes a permanent
  limitation on the owner and the owner's assignees or successors in
  interest from constructing or operating a data center that uses a
  cooling system other than a system or combination of systems
  described by Paragraph (A), and file for recording in the real
  property records of the county in which the real property is located
  the instrument of conveyance of the interest; and
                     (G)  comply with any standards adopted by the
  commission under Section 39.920, Utilities Code, limiting light and
  noise pollution caused by operation of the data center or
  regulating the minimum distance of any component building of the
  data center to the boundary of the data center;
               (2)  authorize the municipality to terminate the
  agreement if the owner or lessee, as applicable, fails to comply
  with a term of the agreement; and
               (3)  require the municipality to recover from the owner
  or lessee, as applicable, a penalty in an amount equal to all lost
  ad valorem tax revenue from the data center and interest on that
  amount calculated as provided by Section 111.060 if the agreement
  is terminated.
         (d)  An agreement is not required to provide for the
  conveyance of a nonpossessory interest in real property as provided
  by Subsection (c)(1)(F) if the owner has conveyed an interest in the
  property to:
               (1)  another taxing unit under a separate agreement
  entered into under this chapter;
               (2)  a municipality or county under an agreement
  entered into under Chapter 380 or 381, Local Government Code; or
               (3)  a school district under an agreement entered into
  under Subchapter T, Chapter 403, Government Code.
         SECTION 9.  Section 312.206(a), Tax Code, is amended to read
  as follows:
         (a)  If property taxes on property located in the taxing

         SECTION 10.  Section 312.402(a-2), Tax Code, is amended to
  read as follows:
         (a-2)  The execution, duration, and other terms of an

         SECTION 11.  Subchapter H, Chapter 39, Utilities Code, is
  amended by adding Section 39.362 to read as follows:
         Sec. 39.362.  DATA CENTER LARGE LOAD REGISTRATION. (a)  This
  section applies only to a large load customer who is subject to the
  standards adopted under Section 37.0561 and intends to interconnect
  or has interconnected to the ERCOT transmission grid a site that
  includes one or more facilities that:
               (1)  individually or collectively encompass at least
  100,000 square feet of space; and
               (2)  are specifically constructed or refurbished to
  house servers and related equipment and support staff for the
  processing, storage, and distribution of data.
         (b)  The commission by rule shall:
               (1)  require a large load customer to register as a
  large load the facility or facilities receiving electric service at
  the data center; and
               (2)  establish a method to ensure compliance with this
  section.
         SECTION 12.  Subchapter Z, Chapter 39, Utilities Code, is
  amended by adding Section 39.920 to read as follows:
         Sec. 39.920.  DATA CENTER CUSTOMERS. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Closed-cycle recirculating system" means a
  cooling system that uses a minimized volume of water to support
  contact or no-contact cooling uses in one or more facilities by
  passing water through a condenser, a sub-surface zone, an adiabatic
  chamber, or another component of the system and reusing that water
  for cooling multiple times without adding new water to the system,
  except as necessary to replenish losses caused by blowdown, drift,
  or leakage.
               (2)  "Data center customer" means a customer registered
  under Section 39.362.
               (3)  "Dry cooling system" means a cooling system that
  uses no water to support contact or no-contact cooling uses in one
  or more facilities.
               (4)  "Saline water cooling system" means a cooling
  system that is determined by the commission to use only desalinated
  seawater, desalinated brackish water, or water derived from fluid
  oil and gas waste, as defined by Section 122.001, Natural Resources
  Code, for cooling purposes.
               (5)  "Water-efficient cooling system" means a cooling
  system that is determined by the commission to use less total water
  for cooling purposes per unit of power consumed by the system than a
  closed-cycle recirculating system would use in the same or
  substantially similar circumstances.
         (b)  The commission by rule shall require a data center
  customer to, before interconnecting the customer's site to the
  ERCOT transmission grid:
               (1)  use only a closed-cycle recirculating system, dry
  cooling system, saline water cooling system, water-efficient
  cooling system, or a combination of such systems to cool the
  facility or facilities on the customer's site, including any
  generation resource co-located on the site; and
               (2)  submit documentation in a form prescribed by the
  commission that:
                     (A)  estimates the customer's anticipated annual
  water supply needs for cooling purposes on the customer's site; and
                     (B)  states the anticipated source or sources of
  water the customer will procure to meet the customer's anticipated
  annual water supply needs.
         (c)  The commission by rule shall require a data center
  customer to, after interconnecting the customer's site to the ERCOT
  transmission grid, annually submit to the commission documentation
  in a form prescribed by the commission that states for the preceding
  calendar year:
               (1)  the customer's water usage for cooling purposes;
  and
               (2)  the source or sources of water used by the customer
  to meet the customer's water needs.
         (d)  At least once each year, the commission shall provide to
  the Texas Water Development Board the documentation provided to the
  commission under Subsections (b) and (c) during the preceding year.
         (e)  The commission shall consult with the Texas Water
  Development Board in developing the forms required by Subsections
  (b) and (c).
         (f)  The commission by rule shall:
               (1)  prohibit an electric cooperative, municipally
  owned utility, or transmission and distribution utility from
  interconnecting a data center customer's site to the ERCOT
  transmission grid, unless:
                     (A)  the site uses only a closed-cycle
  recirculating system, dry cooling system, saline water cooling
  system, water-efficient cooling system, or combination of those
  systems at the time of interconnection; and
                     (B)  the customer has submitted to the commission
  the documentation required by Subsection (b); and
               (2)  require an electric cooperative, municipally
  owned utility, or transmission and distribution utility to
  disconnect a customer's campus from the ERCOT transmission grid if
  the customer:
                     (A)  uses a cooling system other than a
  closed-cycle recirculating system, dry cooling system, saline
  water cooling system, water-efficient cooling system, or
  combination of those systems to cool a facility located on the
  customer's campus, including any generation resource co-located on
  the campus, while interconnected to the grid; or
                     (B)  fails to timely submit to the commission the
  documentation required by Subsection (c).
         (g)  The commission by rule may require a data center
  customer to:
               (1)  limit the noise and light pollution caused by the
  operation of any facility on the data center customer's site,
  including any noise and light pollution caused by a generation
  resource co-located on the customer's site; and
               (2)  locate a facility on the customer's site,
  including any generation resource co-located on the customer's
  site, a minimum distance from the boundary of the tract of land that
  comprises the site.
         (h)  The commission by rule may:
               (1)  prohibit an electric cooperative, municipally
  owned utility, or transmission and distribution utility from
  interconnecting a data center customer's site to the ERCOT
  transmission grid because the customer's site is not in compliance
  with the standards adopted by the commission under Subsection (g);
  and
               (2)  require an electric cooperative, municipally
  owned utility, or transmission and distribution utility to
  disconnect a data center customer's site from the ERCOT
  transmission grid because the site is not in compliance with the
  standards adopted by the commission under Subsection (g).
         SECTION 13.  Subchapter L, Chapter 5, Water Code, is amended
  by adding Section 5.517 to read as follows:
         Sec. 5.517.  EMERGENCY ORDER CONCERNING CONSTRUCTION
  ACTIVITY OF DATA CENTER. The commission shall issue an emergency
  order in the manner provided by Subchapter L, Chapter 5, requiring
  the cessation of construction of a data center authorized under a
  general permit issued under Section 26.040 that authorizes
  discharges of stormwater runoff and non-stormwater discharges from
  construction if the commission determines, after receiving a
  complaint or learning through other means, that the data center
  does not or will not comply with the standards and rules described
  by Section 26.054(c).
         SECTION 14.  Subchapter B, Chapter 26, Water Code, is
  amended by adding Section 26.054 to read as follows:
         Sec. 26.054.  PERMIT CONDITIONS FOR DATA CENTERS. (a)  In
  this section, "data center" means a site that has one or more
  facilities that encompass at least 100,000 square feet of space and
  are specifically constructed or refurbished to house servers and
  related equipment and support staff for the processing, storage,
  and distribution of data.
         (b)  This section applies only to a general permit issued
  under Section 26.040 that authorizes discharges of stormwater
  runoff and non-stormwater discharges from construction activities.
         (c)  An applicant for authorization to discharge waste from a
  data center under a general permit shall attest in the notice of
  intent submitted to the commission that the applicant is compliant
  or will comply with applicable standards and rules adopted by the
  Public Utility Commission of Texas under Sections 37.0561 and
  39.920, Utilities Code.
         (d)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter,
  the commission, after hearing, shall deny or suspend a discharger's
  authority to discharge from a data center under a general permit if
  the commission determines that the data center does not or will not
  comply with applicable standards and rules described by Subsection
  (c).
         SECTION 15.  Sections 403.605(a) and 403.612(d), Government
  Code, as amended by this Act, and Section 403.6121, Government
  Code, as added by this Act, apply only to an agreement limiting the
  taxable value of property entered into under Subchapter T, Chapter
  403, Government Code, on or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 16.  Section 380.005, Local Government Code, as
  added by this Act, applies only to a loan or grant of public money
  made on or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 17.  Section 381.004(g), Local Government Code, as
  amended by this Act, applies only to an agreement entered into on or
  after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 18.  Sections 312.204(a-1) and 312.2042, Tax Code,
  as added by this Act, and Sections 312.206(a) and 312.402(a-2), Tax
  Code, as amended by this Act, apply only to a tax abatement
  agreement entered into on or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 19.  The Public Utility Commission of Texas may
  apply rules adopted under Section 39.920, Utilities Code, as added
  by this Act, only to the site of a data center customer
  interconnected to the ERCOT transmission grid on or after the
  effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 20.  Section 26.054, Water Code, as added by this
  Act, applies only to an application for authorization to use a
  general permit that is filed with the Texas Commission on
  Environmental Quality on or after the effective date of this Act. An
  application for authorization to use a general permit that is filed
  before the effective date of this Act is governed by the law in
  effect on the date of filing, and that law is continued in effect
  for that purpose.
         SECTION 21.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
