A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the recovery of costs related to providing electricity
  service to customers at a computational load site.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Sections 35.004(c-1) and (c-2), Utilities Code,
  are redesignated as Section 35.0041, Utilities Code, and amended to
  read as follows:
         Sec. 35.0041.  INTERCONNECTION OF AND COST RECOVERY FROM
  LARGE LOAD CUSTOMERS. (a) [(c-1)]  The commission by rule shall
  ensure that a large load customer who is subject to the standards
  adopted under Section 37.0561 contributes to the recovery of the
  interconnecting electric utility's costs to interconnect the large
  load to the utility's system.
         (b) [(c-2)]  An electric cooperative or municipally owned
  utility that has not adopted customer choice shall pass through to a
  large load customer who is subject to the standards adopted under
  Section 37.0561 the reasonable costs to interconnect the large load
  in a manner determined by the electric cooperative or municipally
  owned utility.
         (c)  This subsection applies only to costs not recovered
  under Subsection (a) or (b). Notwithstanding Section 35.004, the
  commission by rule shall establish a system to:
               (1)  ensure that transmission costs of service
  attributable to a customer receiving electricity service at a
  computational load site are allocated to a separate transmission
  cost of service rate class composed only of that type of customer;
  and
               (2)  require each electric utility that provides
  distribution service to a customer described by this subsection to
  use a rate mechanism established by the commission for the purposes
  of Subdivision (1).
         (d)  For purposes of Subsection (c), "computational load
  site" means one or more buildings, structures, or installations at
  a single location that:
               (1)  primarily contains information technology
  infrastructure used for:
                     (A)  creating, executing, and delivering software
  applications and services;
                     (B)  artificial intelligence training,
  deployment, and delivery;
                     (C)  adding virtual currency transactions to a
  distributed ledger; or
                     (D)  other computational activities at a data
  center; and
               (2)  is supplied through electrical equipment that is
  connected to the bulk power system.
         (e)  In this section, "virtual currency" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 12.001, Business & Commerce Code.
         (f)  The system established under Subsection (c) may include
  requirements that customers subject to that subsection pay minimum
  demand charges, operate under contract terms specified by the
  commission, or meet any other requirements the commission
  determines necessary to ensure that transmission costs of service
  attributable to those customers are not paid by other customers.
         SECTION 2.  The Public Utility Commission of Texas shall
  adopt or revise rules as necessary to implement the changes in law
  made by this Act not later than September 1, 2028.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
