A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to automated external defibrillators at high-occupancy
  facilities and mass gatherings.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 779, Health and Safety Code, is amended
  by adding Sections 779.009 and 779.010 to read as follows:
         Sec. 779.009.  MANDATORY AUTOMATED EXTERNAL DEFIBRILLATOR
  AT HIGH-OCCUPANCY FACILITY.  (a)  In this section, "high-occupancy
  facility" means a building or facility that has an occupancy
  capacity of at least 100 individuals and is open to the public or
  regularly used for public or private gatherings.  The term includes
  an event venue, banquet hall, gymnasium, fitness center, theater,
  place of assembly, and any other similar building or facility. 
         (b)  A person who owns or operates a high-occupancy facility
  shall ensure at least one automated external defibrillator is
  readily available at the facility.  The person shall:
               (1)  place the defibrillator in a conspicuous and
  easily accessible location; 
               (2)  post signs clearly indicating the location of the
  defibrillator; 
               (3)  ensure the defibrillator is available while the
  facility is open to the public or occupied; and
               (4)  maintain and inspect the defibrillator in
  accordance with Section 779.003. 
         (c)  The executive commissioner of the Health and Human
  Services Commission may adopt rules necessary to implement this
  section. 
         Sec. 779.010.  MANDATORY AUTOMATED EXTERNAL DEFIBRILLATOR
  AT MASS GATHERINGS. (a)  This section applies only to an organizer
  of a public or private gathering at which at least 100 individuals
  are reasonably expected to attend. 
         (b)  An organizer shall ensure at least one automated
  external defibrillator is readily available at the location at
  which a gathering described by Subsection (a) is held. The
  organizer shall:
               (1)  place the defibrillator in a conspicuous and
  easily accessible area of the location; 
               (2)  ensure the defibrillator is available throughout
  the duration of the gathering; and
               (3)  ensure the defibrillator has been maintained and
  inspected in accordance with Section 779.003. 
         (c)  This section does not require an organizer to provide an
  automated external defibrillator if the location at which a
  gathering is held provides access to a defibrillator that meets the
  requirements of this section.
         (d)  The executive commissioner of the Health and Human
  Services Commission may adopt rules necessary to implement this
  section. 
         SECTION 2.  (a) Not later than December 1, 2027, the
  executive commissioner of the Health and Human Services Commission
  shall adopt rules necessary to implement the changes in law made by
  this Act.
         (b)  Notwithstanding Sections 779.009 and 779.010, Health
  and Safety Code, as added by this Act, a person is not required to
  comply with those sections until January 1, 2028.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
