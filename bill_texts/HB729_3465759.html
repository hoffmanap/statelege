By: Perez of El Paso H.B. No. 729
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to limitations on the duration of and the property covered
  by certain local tax abatement agreements and economic development
  program agreements, including limitations applicable to additional
  phases of a project.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 312.007, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Subsections (c), (d), and (e) to read as follows:
         (c)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter and
  regardless of the number of abatement periods provided for by an
  agreement, the aggregate duration of all abatement periods provided
  for by one or more tax abatement agreements between a taxing unit
  and the same owner of property, or an affiliate or successor in
  interest of that owner, that cover property located on the same
  parcel or tract of real property, or on land subsequently added to
  that parcel or tract, may not exceed 10 years.  The aggregate
  duration is computed beginning with the first year of the first
  abatement period under the earliest of those agreements.
         (d)  A provision of a tax abatement agreement that provides
  for a separate abatement period for a building, a portion of a
  building, a group of buildings, improvements, or tangible personal
  property designated as a phase or other unit of a project is void to
  the extent the provision would result in an aggregate duration of
  abatement periods that exceeds the period permitted by Subsection
  (c).
         (e)  An owner or lessee of property may not receive an
  exemption from taxation of any portion of the value of the property
  under a tax abatement agreement for a tax year that begins after the
  expiration of the period permitted by Subsection (c).
         SECTION 2.  Subchapter A, Chapter 312, Tax Code, is amended
  by adding Section 312.0023 to read as follows:
         Sec. 312.0023.  APPROVAL REQUIRED FOR ABATEMENT OF VALUE OF
  PHASE OF PROJECT. (a)  In this section, "phase" means a building, a
  portion of a building, a group of buildings, improvements, or
  tangible personal property that is designated, or that under the
  terms of a tax abatement agreement may be designated, as a unit of a
  project for which a separate exemption from taxation or a separate
  abatement period may be provided.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, an
  owner or lessee of property may not receive an exemption from
  taxation of any portion of the value of a phase under a tax
  abatement agreement unless, on or after the date construction of
  the improvements comprising that phase commenced, the governing
  body of the taxing unit has approved the exemption of the value of
  that phase by the affirmative vote of a majority of the members of
  the governing body at a regularly scheduled meeting of the
  governing body for which notice was provided in the manner required
  by Sections 312.207(c) and (d).
         (c)  A taxing unit may not enter into, or maintain in effect,
  a tax abatement agreement that provides for an exemption from
  taxation of any portion of the value of a phase for which the
  approval described by Subsection (b) has not been given.
         (d)  An approval described by Subsection (b) may not be given
  before the date construction of the improvements comprising the
  phase commences, and a taxing unit may not agree in advance to give
  that approval.
         SECTION 3.  Section 312.205, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Subsections (c), (d), and (e) to read as follows:
         (c)  The list required by Subsection (a)(1) must describe, by
  kind, number, and location, each improvement to the property for
  which an exemption from taxation may be provided under the
  agreement, including each improvement that is part of a phase or
  other unit of a project for which a separate exemption or a separate
  abatement period may be provided.  An agreement may not provide for
  an exemption of the value of an improvement or of tangible personal
  property that is not described in the agreement as approved by the
  governing body under Section 312.207.
         (d)  A provision of an agreement that purports to authorize
  the owner of the property, or a person other than the governing body
  of the taxing unit, to take any of the following actions without the
  approval of the governing body in the manner required by Sections
  312.207 and 312.208 is void:
               (1)  designate a phase or other unit of a project for
  which a separate exemption from taxation or a separate abatement
  period may be provided;
               (2)  add real property, an improvement, or tangible
  personal property to the property covered by the agreement,
  including property located on land acquired after the date the
  agreement is approved;
               (3)  adjust a boundary described in the agreement; or
               (4)  extend the term of the agreement or an abatement
  period.
         (e)  An action described by Subsection (d) is a modification
  of the agreement for purposes of Section 312.208 and must be
  approved and executed in the manner required by that section.
  Notice of the meeting at which the governing body will consider the
  modification must be provided in the manner required by Sections
  312.207(c) and (d).
         SECTION 4.  Chapter 380, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 380.006 to read as follows:
         Sec. 380.006.  LIMITATIONS ON PROGRAM BENEFITS FOR PHASES OF
  PROJECT. (a)  In this section, "phase" means a building, a portion
  of a building, a group of buildings, improvements, or tangible
  personal property that is designated, or that under the terms of an
  agreement entered into in connection with a program established
  under Section 380.001 may be designated, as a unit of a project for
  which a separate loan, grant, or other payment or a separate period
  of payments may be provided.
         (b)  A municipality may not make a loan or grant of public
  money or provide any other benefit under a program established
  under Section 380.001 with respect to a phase unless, on or after
  the date construction of the improvements comprising that phase
  commenced, the governing body of the municipality has approved the
  loan, grant, or other benefit for that phase by the affirmative vote
  of a majority of the members of the governing body at an open
  meeting held in accordance with Chapter 551, Government Code.  The
  governing body may not give that approval before the date
  construction of those improvements commences and may not agree in
  advance to give that approval.
         (c)  A provision of an agreement entered into in connection
  with a program established under Section 380.001 that purports to
  authorize a person other than the governing body of the
  municipality to designate a phase, to add real property, an
  improvement, or tangible personal property to the property covered
  by the agreement, to adjust a boundary described in the agreement,
  or to extend the term of the agreement or a period of payments, in
  each case without the approval described by Subsection (b), is
  void.
         (d)  The aggregate period for which a municipality may make
  payments under one or more agreements entered into in connection
  with a program established under Section 380.001 with the same
  person, or an affiliate or successor in interest of that person,
  that are measured in whole or in part by ad valorem taxes imposed on
  property located on the same parcel or tract of real property, or on
  land subsequently added to that parcel or tract, may not exceed 10
  years.  The aggregate period is computed beginning with the first
  year for which a payment under the earliest of those agreements is
  made.
         SECTION 5.  Chapter 381, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 381.007 to read as follows:
         Sec. 381.007.  LIMITATIONS ON PROGRAM BENEFITS FOR PHASES OF
  PROJECT. (a)  In this section, "phase" means a building, a portion
  of a building, a group of buildings, improvements, or tangible
  personal property that is designated, or that under the terms of an
  agreement entered into in connection with a program developed under
  Section 381.004 may be designated, as a unit of a project for which
  a separate loan, grant, or other payment or a separate period of
  payments may be provided.
         (b)  A commissioners court may not make a loan or grant of
  public money or provide any other benefit under a program developed
  under Section 381.004 with respect to a phase unless, on or after
  the date construction of the improvements comprising that phase
  commenced, the commissioners court has approved the loan, grant, or
  other benefit for that phase by the affirmative vote of a majority
  of the members of the commissioners court at an open meeting held in
  accordance with Chapter 551, Government Code.  The commissioners
  court may not give that approval before the date construction of
  those improvements commences and may not agree in advance to give
  that approval.
         (c)  A provision of an agreement entered into in connection
  with a program developed under Section 381.004 that purports to
  authorize a person other than the commissioners court to designate
  a phase, to add real property, an improvement, or tangible personal
  property to the property covered by the agreement, to adjust a
  boundary described in the agreement, or to extend the term of the
  agreement or a period of payments, in each case without the approval
  described by Subsection (b), is void.
         (d)  The aggregate period for which a county may make
  payments under one or more agreements entered into in connection
  with a program developed under Section 381.004 with the same
  person, or an affiliate or successor in interest of that person,
  that are measured in whole or in part by ad valorem taxes imposed on
  property located on the same parcel or tract of real property, or on
  land subsequently added to that parcel or tract, may not exceed 10
  years.  The aggregate period is computed beginning with the first
  year for which a payment under the earliest of those agreements is
  made.
         SECTION 6.  (a)  Except as provided by Subsection (b) of this
  section, the changes in law made by this Act apply only to an
  agreement entered into on or after the effective date of this Act.
         (b)  Sections 312.007(c), (d), and (e), 312.0023, and
  312.205(c), (d), and (e), Tax Code, as added by this Act, and
  Sections 380.006 and 381.007, Local Government Code, as added by
  this Act, apply to an agreement entered into before the effective
  date of this Act with respect to:
               (1)  a phase designated on or after the effective date
  of this Act;
               (2)  property added to the property covered by the
  agreement on or after the effective date of this Act;
               (3)  an abatement period or period of payments that had
  not commenced before the effective date of this Act; and
               (4)  a tax year that begins on or after the effective
  date of this Act.
         (c)  The changes in law made by this Act do not affect:
               (1)  an exemption from taxation for a tax year that
  began before the effective date of this Act;
               (2)  an abatement period that commenced before the
  effective date of this Act, for the duration of that abatement
  period as provided by the agreement on the effective date of this
  Act;
               (3)  a payment that a municipality or county became
  obligated to make before the effective date of this Act; or
               (4)  the first phase designated under an agreement
  entered into before the effective date of this Act, if the
  improvements comprising that phase are described in the agreement
  as approved by the governing body of the taxing unit, municipality,
  or county before the effective date of this Act.
         (d)  Not later than the 90th day after the effective date of
  this Act, a taxing unit, municipality, or county that is a party to
  an agreement entered into before the effective date of this Act that
  contains a provision that is void under this Act shall provide
  written notice of that fact to each other party to the agreement and
  to the comptroller of public accounts.
         (e)  A provision of an agreement entered into before the
  effective date of this Act that conflicts with this Act is void to
  the extent of the conflict.
         SECTION 7.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
