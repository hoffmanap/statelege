A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to requirements and preferences for purchasing
  construction materials produced in the United States and this
  state.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 2252, Government Code, is amended by
  adding Subchapter H to read as follows:
  SUBCHAPTER H. REQUIREMENTS AND PREFERENCES FOR DOMESTIC
  CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS
         Sec. 2252.231.  DEFINITIONS. In this subchapter:
               (1)  "Construction materials" means, if permanently
  incorporated into a public infrastructure project, iron, steel,
  manufactured products, concrete, cement, aggregates, glass,
  lumber, drywall, copper, aluminum, asphalt, plastics, and
  fiber-optic cable.
               (2)  "Political subdivision" includes a county, a
  municipality, a municipal utility district, a water control and
  improvement district, a special utility district, and other types
  of water districts.
               (3)  "Procuring entity" means a:
                     (A)  state agency; or 
                     (B)  political subdivision.
               (4)  "Produced in the United States" means: 
                     (A)  with respect to iron and steel, all
  manufacturing processes, from the initial melting stage through the
  application of coatings, occurred in the United States;
                     (B)  with respect to a manufactured product: 
                           (i)  the product was manufactured in the
  United States; and
                           (ii)  the cost of the product's components
  that are mined, produced, or manufactured in the United States is
  greater than 55 percent of the total cost of all the product's
  components; and
                     (C)  with respect to a construction material other
  than iron, steel, or a manufactured product, all manufacturing
  processes for the material occurred in the United States.
               (5)  "Public infrastructure project" means the
  construction, reconstruction, alteration, repair, maintenance, or
  improvement of any building, structure, or infrastructure by a
  procuring entity through a contract with another person. The term
  includes any:
                     (A)  road, highway, or bridge; 
                     (B)  water, wastewater, or flood control
  facility;
                     (C)  public building or facility;
                     (D)  transit or rail facility, port, or airport;
  or
                     (E)  broadband, telecommunications, or utility
  infrastructure.
               (6)  "State agency" has the meaning assigned by Section
  2151.002.
         Sec. 2252.232.  DUTY TO REQUIRE PURCHASE OF CONSTRUCTION
  MATERIALS PRODUCED IN UNITED STATES. Except as provided by Section
  2252.235, for each public infrastructure project of a procuring
  entity with an estimated contract value of $1 million or more, the
  procuring entity shall require the bid documents provided to all
  bidders and the resulting contract to include a provision requiring
  any construction materials used in the project to be produced in the
  United States.
         Sec. 2252.233.  POWER TO REQUIRE PURCHASE OF CONSTRUCTION
  MATERIALS PRODUCED IN UNITED STATES. For each public
  infrastructure project by a procuring entity with an estimated
  contract value of $1 million or less, the procuring entity may
  require the bid documents provided to all bidders and the resulting
  contract to include a provision requiring any construction
  materials used in the project to be produced in the United States.
         Sec. 2252.234.  PREFERENCE FOR CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS
  PRODUCED IN TEXAS. (a) A state agency that as a procuring entity
  includes a contract provision under Section 2252.232 or 2252.233
  shall also include a contract provision requiring the contractor to
  purchase products and materials in the manner provided by Section
  2155.444.
         (b)  A political subdivision that as a procuring entity
  includes a contract provision under Section 2252.232 or 2252.233
  may also include the contract provision described by Subsection
  (a). 
         Sec. 2252.235.  DETERMINATION FOR EXEMPTION BASED ON
  NONAVAILABILITY, DELAY, OR COST. (a) Section 2252.232 does not
  apply to a public infrastructure project of a procuring entity if
  the procuring entity determines in writing that:
               (1)  the construction materials needed for the public
  infrastructure project and produced in the United States are not: 
                     (A)  reasonably available in sufficient
  quantities; or
                     (B)  of satisfactory quality; or
               (2)  compliance with Section 2252.232 would:
                     (A)  materially delay the project schedule; or
                     (B)  result in unreasonable cost increases.
         (b)  A determination under this section is not subject to
  public disclosure under Chapter 552. A procuring entity shall
  retain the reasoning for a determination under this section and
  provide the information to the comptroller for inclusion in the
  report required by Section 2252.239.
         Sec. 2252.236.  EXEMPTIONS FOR DISASTER-RELATED AND
  FEDERALLY FUNDED PURCHASES. This subchapter does not apply to a
  contract:
               (1)  entered into in response to a state of disaster
  declared under Chapter 418; or
               (2)  involving federal funds.
         Sec. 2252.237.  CONFLICT WITH OTHER STATE LAWS. To the
  extent of any conflict between this subchapter and another law of
  this state, including Subchapter G, the more stringent law
  controls. 
         Sec. 2252.238.  RULES. The comptroller shall adopt rules to
  administer this subchapter, including rules for ensuring
  contractor compliance.
         Sec. 2252.239.  REPORT. (a) On or before September 1 of
  each year, the comptroller shall collect information from procuring
  entities on the purchase of construction materials under a public
  infrastructure project contract, including:
               (1)  the source of the purchases; and
               (2)  for a contract exempted from this subchapter under
  Section 2252.235, the reasoning supporting the exemption.
         (b)  On or before December 1 of each year, the comptroller
  shall submit a summary report of the information collected under
  Subsection (a) to the legislature.
         SECTION 2.  Not later than December 1, 2027, the comptroller
  shall adopt rules necessary to implement Subchapter H, Chapter
  2252, Government Code, as added by this Act.
         SECTION 3.  Subchapter H, Chapter 2252, Government Code, as
  added by this Act, applies only in relation to a contract for which
  the request for bids or proposals or other applicable expression of
  interest is made public on or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
