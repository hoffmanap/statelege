By: Ochoa H.B. No. 696
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to prohibiting certain governmental entities from
  entering into an agreement prohibiting the entity from releasing
  certain information regarding a data center project.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subtitle Z, Title 10, Government Code, is
  amended by adding Chapter 3003 to read as follows:
  CHAPTER 3003. NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT REGARDING DATA CENTERS
         Sec. 3003.001.  DEFINITIONS. In this chapter:
               (1)  "Data center" means a single building or portion
  of a single building that:
                     (A)  is located in this state;
                     (B)  is specifically constructed or refurbished
  and actually used primarily to house servers and related equipment
  and support staff for the processing, storage, and distribution of
  data;
                     (C)  is used for the processing, storage, and
  distribution of data;
                     (D)  is not used primarily by a telecommunications
  provider to place tangible personal property that is used to
  deliver telecommunications services; and
                     (E)  has an uninterruptible power source,
  generator backup power, a sophisticated fire suppression and
  prevention system, and enhanced physical security that includes
  restricted access, video surveillance, and electronic systems.
               (2)  "Governmental entity" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 2007.002.
         Sec. 3003.002.  NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT PROHIBITED.
  Notwithstanding any other law, a governmental entity may not enter
  into an agreement with a private entity that prohibits the
  governmental entity from disclosing information regarding the
  financing, construction, operation, management, maintenance,
  acquisition, or sale of a data center project.
         SECTION 2.  Chapter 3003, Government Code, as added by this
  Act, applies only to an agreement entered into on or after the
  effective date of this Act. An agreement entered into before the
  effective date of this Act is governed by the law in effect on the
  date the agreement was entered into, and the former law is continued
  in effect for that purpose.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
