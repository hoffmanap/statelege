S.B. No. 1184
 
 
 
 
AN ACT
  relating to the age of wine authorized to be sold by a wine
  collection seller.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 111.002, Alcoholic Beverage Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 111.002.  AUTHORIZED ACTIVITIES. A wine collection
  seller may sell to a permitted restaurant wine that is:
               (1)  lawfully owned or possessed by the wine collection
  seller;
               (2)  in an original container sealed by the
  manufacturer; and
               (3)  manufactured not less than 10 [20] years before
  the date the wine collection seller sells the wine to a permitted
  restaurant.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
