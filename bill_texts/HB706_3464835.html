By: Ochoa H.B. No. 706
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to election and public hearing requirements before a
  municipality may enter into a data center development agreement
  under certain economic development programs.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 380, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 380.005 to read as follows:
         Sec. 380.005.  DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENTS FOR DATA CENTERS;
  ELECTION; PUBLIC HEARING.  (a)  In this section, "data center" means
  at least 100,000 square feet of space in a building or portion of a
  building located in this state, in which the space:
               (1)  is specifically constructed or refurbished and
  actually used primarily to house servers and related equipment and
  support staff for the processing, storage, and distribution of
  data;
               (2)  is used by one or more occupants for the
  processing, storage, and distribution of data;
               (3)  is not used primarily by a telecommunications
  provider to deliver telecommunications services, as defined by
  Section 151.0103, Tax Code; and
               (4)  has an uninterruptible power source, generator
  backup power, and a fire suppression and prevention system.
         (b)  This section applies only to an area that:
               (1)  is located in a municipality;
               (2)  has been annexed by a municipality for limited
  purposes; or
               (3)  is located in the extraterritorial jurisdiction of
  a municipality.
         (c)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter,
  the governing body of a municipality, or an entity with which the
  governing body may contract to administer a program under this
  chapter, may not enter into a development agreement under an
  economic development program established by this chapter if the
  subject of the agreement is the construction or operation of a data
  center in an area to which this section applies unless:
               (1)  the governing body of the municipality holds not
  less than five public hearings on the subject of the development
  agreement; and
               (2)  the voters of an area to which this section applies
  support entering into the development agreement at an election held
  for that purpose.
         (d)  The governing body of a municipality, in relation to a
  public hearing held under this section, must:
               (1)  provide public notice for each hearing that:
                     (A)  is provided not earlier than the seventh day
  before the date of each public hearing;
                     (B)  is provided in a newspaper or other
  publication regularly circulated in the municipality; and
                     (C)  is posted on:
                           (i)  the municipality's Internet website;
                           (ii)  any social media account managed by
  the municipality; and
                           (iii)  each polling place where the
  municipality intends to hold an election under Subsection (c);
               (2)  provide interested parties an opportunity to be
  heard on the development agreement at the hearing; and
               (3)  invite the following persons to the hearing:
                     (A)  residents of an area to which this section
  applies;
                     (B)  the person entering into the development
  agreement with the municipality;
                     (C)  any department of the municipality that would
  be impacted by the development, including departments related to
  electricity and water use, municipal infrastructure, and housing;
  and
                     (D)  any business located in an area to which this
  section applies that would be impacted by the development
  agreement, including retail electricity providers if applicable.
         (e)  The governing body of a municipality may only order an
  election under Subsection (c):
               (1)  on a uniform election date; and
               (2)  after:
                     (A)  the governing body holds the public hearings
  as provided by this section; and
                     (B)  providing notice of the election not earlier
  than the 90th day before the date of the election:
                           (i)  in a newspaper or other publication
  regularly circulated in the municipality;
                           (ii)  on the municipality's Internet
  website;
                           (iii)  on any social media account managed
  by the municipality; and
                           (iv)  at each polling place where the
  municipality intends to hold the election.
         (f)  A ballot for an election ordered under Subsection (c)
  must be printed to provide for voting for or against the
  proposition:  "Granting authority to the municipality or
  municipality's contracting entity to enter into a development
  agreement for the purposes of constructing or operating a data
  center."  Each qualified voter of an area to which this section
  applies is entitled to vote in the election.
         SECTION 2.  Section 380.005, Local Government Code, as added
  by this Act, applies only to a contract or agreement entered into on
  or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
