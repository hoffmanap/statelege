H.B. No. 2763
 
 
 
 
AN ACT
  relating to the designation of a Texas Department of Transportation
  building in Starr County as the Eduardo "Eddie" Gracia, Jr., P.E.,
  Area Engineer and Maintenance Facility.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter Y, Chapter 201, Transportation Code,
  is amended by adding Section 201.2004 to read as follows:
         Sec. 201.2004.  EDUARDO "EDDIE" GRACIA, JR., P.E., AREA
  ENGINEER AND MAINTENANCE FACILITY. (a) The building in which, on
  January 1, 2025, is located the office of the area engineer for
  Starr County is designated as the Eduardo "Eddie" Gracia, Jr.,
  P.E., Area Engineer and Maintenance Facility.
         (b)  Subject to Subsection (c), the department shall:
               (1)  design and construct markers at the site of the
  facility indicating the designation as the Eduardo "Eddie" Gracia,
  Jr., P.E., Area Engineer and Maintenance Facility and any other
  appropriate information; and
               (2)  update existing signage along the highway on which
  the facility is located to reflect the designation described by
  Subdivision (1).
         (c)  The department is not required to design or construct a
  marker or update existing signage under this section unless a grant
  or donation of private funds is made to the department to cover the
  cost of the design and construction of the marker or update of the
  existing signage.
         (d)  Money received under Subsection (c) shall be deposited
  to the credit of the state highway fund.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
