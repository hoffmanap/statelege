By: Zaffirini S.B. No. 32
 
 
 
   
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the issuance by the Public Utilities Commission of
  Texas of a permit for the construction of a large computational load
  site; providing an administrative penalty.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  The Utilities Code is amended by adding Title 7
  to read as follows:
  TITLE 7.  LARGE COMPUTATIONAL LOAD SITES
  CHAPTER 401.  LARGE COMPUTATIONAL LOAD SITES
         Sec. 401.001.  DEFINITIONS.  In this chapter:
               (1)  "Commission" means the Public Utility Commission
  of Texas.
               (2)  "Computational load site" means one or more
  buildings, structures, or installations at a single location that:
                     (A)  primarily contains information technology
  infrastructure used for:
                           (i)  creating, executing, and delivering
  software applications and services;
                           (ii)  artificial intelligence training,
  deployment, and delivery;
                           (iii)  adding virtual currency transactions
  to a distributed ledger; or
                           (iv)  other computational activities at a
  data center; and
                     (B)  is supplied through electrical equipment
  that is connected to the bulk power system.
               (3)  "Environmental commission" means the Texas
  Commission on Environmental Quality.
               (4)  "ERCOT" has the meaning assigned by Section
  31.002.
               (5)  "Large computational load site" means a
  computational load site that has or is expected to have an aggregate
  minimum electric power demand of at least 75 megawatts.
               (6)  "Virtual currency" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 12.001, Business & Commerce Code.
         Sec. 401.002.  PERMIT REQUIRED BEFORE BEGINNING
  CONSTRUCTION. (a) The commission, in collaboration with the
  environmental commission, by rule shall require a permit for the
  operation of a large computational load site before any
  construction, including construction for a material expansion,
  redevelopment, or change in the use of a facility, begins at the
  site.
         (b)  An application for a permit issued under this section
  must include:
               (1)  a description of the proposed location of the
  site; and
               (2)  any other information required by commission rule,
  which may include:
                     (A)  a copy of any documentation submitted under
  Section 37.0561 for planning purposes for a site in the ERCOT power
  region;
                     (B)  information regarding the site's anticipated
  electric demand, schedule for energization, electric load growth,
  and electric infrastructure;
                     (C)  water supply, wastewater, drainage,
  floodplain, and infrastructure impact analyses;
                     (D)  an identification of the proposed water
  source for the site and information regarding the availability of
  an adequate long-term water supply for the site; and
                     (E)  information reasonably necessary to identify
  and evaluate measures to mitigate potential adverse impacts
  associated with the site and within the jurisdiction of the
  commission or the environmental commission.
         (c)  The commission may hold one or more public meetings on a
  permit application.
         (d)  In considering whether to approve, conditionally
  approve, or deny a permit application filed under this section, the
  commission shall consider:
               (1)  the site's planned water and energy use;
               (2)  the site's potential environmental impacts;
               (3)  the site's potential impacts on power grid
  reliability and the transmission system in this state; and
               (4)  any measures the applicant may take to mitigate
  water, energy, environmental, and power grid impacts.
         (e)  The commission may deny a permit if the applicant fails
  to demonstrate compliance with commission or environmental
  commission rules or fails to adequately address a documented or
  reasonably foreseeable negative impact identified by the
  commission when reviewing the application.
         (f)  After notice and an opportunity for a hearing, the
  commission may suspend or revoke a permit issued under this chapter
  for a violation of this chapter or rule adopted under this chapter.
         (g)  The commission by rule shall adopt a fee to be imposed on
  each permit applicant to cover costs associated with implementing
  this chapter.
         (h)  Information submitted to the commission in an
  application for a permit under this chapter is confidential and not
  subject to disclosure under Chapter 552, Government Code.
         Sec. 401.003.  ENFORCEMENT. For purposes of this chapter, a
  reference in Chapter 15 to a person includes a person required to
  obtain a permit under this chapter and the commission may impose an
  administrative penalty under Section 15.023 on a person who
  violates this chapter or a rule adopted under this chapter.
         SECTION 2.  Section 5.013(a), Water Code, is amended to read
  as follows:
          (a)  The commission has general jurisdiction over:
               (1)  water and water rights including the issuance of
  water rights permits, water rights adjudication, cancellation of
  water rights, and enforcement of water rights;
               (2)  continuing supervision over districts created
  under Article III, Sections 52(b)(1) and (2), and Article XVI,
  Section 59, of the Texas Constitution;
               (3)  the state's water quality program including
  issuance of permits, enforcement of water quality rules, standards,
  orders, and permits, and water quality planning;
               (4)  the determination of the feasibility of certain
  federal projects;
               (5)  the adoption and enforcement of rules and
  performance of other acts relating to the safe construction,
  maintenance, and removal of dams;
               (6)  conduct of the state's hazardous spill prevention
  and control program;
               (7)  the administration of the state's program relating
  to inactive hazardous substance, pollutant, and contaminant
  disposal facilities;
               (8)  the administration of a portion of the state's
  injection well program;
               (9)  the administration of the state's programs
  involving underground water and water wells and drilled and mined
  shafts;
               (10)  the state's responsibilities relating to regional
  waste disposal;
               (11)  the responsibilities assigned to the commission
  by Chapters 361, 363, 376, 382, 401, 505, 506, and 507, Health and
  Safety Code, and Chapter 401, Utilities Code; and
               (12)  any other areas assigned to the commission by
  this code and other laws of this state.
         SECTION 3.  As soon as practicable after the effective date
  of this Act, the Public Utilities Commission of Texas and the Texas
  Commission on Environmental Quality shall:
               (1)  update the memorandum of understanding
  establishing joint jurisdiction between the utilities commission
  and the commission to ensure compliance with Title 7, Utilities
  Code, as added by this Act, and facilitate the sharing of data
  related to large computational load sites; and
               (2)  adopt rules as necessary to implement Title 7,
  Utilities Code, as added by this Act.
         SECTION 4.  Title 7, Utilities Code, as added by this Act,
  applies only a large computational load site for which construction
  begins on or after the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 5.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
