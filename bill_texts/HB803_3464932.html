A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to a Texas Department of Criminal Justice policy regarding
  indoor temperatures at correctional facilities.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter A, Chapter 501, Government Code, is
  amended by adding Section 501.028 to read as follows:
         Sec. 501.028.  POLICY REGARDING INDOOR TEMPERATURE AT
  DEPARTMENT FACILITIES. (a) The department shall adopt a policy
  regarding the temperature in each correctional facility building
  operated by or under contract with the department that is regularly
  used by inmates, department staff, contractors, or visitors. The
  policy must:
               (1)  establish a reasonable indoor temperature range
  that ensures the health and safety of the occupants;
               (2)  ensure the provision of adequate:
                     (A)  air conditioning during extreme heat; and
                     (B)  heating during cold weather;
               (3)  prioritize heating and cooling medical units and
  units housing:
                     (A)  elderly inmates;
                     (B)  disabled inmates;
                     (C)  pregnant inmates; and
                     (D)  any inmates with increased susceptibility to
  extreme temperatures; and
               (4)  establish an emergency protocol for when indoor
  temperatures are outside of the established temperature range,
  including:
                     (A)  ensuring the expedited repair of any
  malfunctioning HVAC systems;
                     (B)  ensuring the provision of:
                           (i)  access to cool water;
                           (ii)  cooling stations;
                           (iii)  warming stations; and
                           (iv)  medical monitoring; and
                     (C)  temporarily relocating inmates.
         (b)  The department shall publish on the department's
  Internet website an implementation plan for any facilities that do
  not comply with the policy adopted under Subsection (a). The plan
  must include the following for each facility:
               (1)  an assessment of alterations or improvements
  necessary to achieve compliance, including the estimated cost; and
               (2)  a timeline for bringing the facility into
  compliance.
         (c)  Not later than December 1 of each year, the department
  shall submit a report to the legislature on the department's
  compliance with the policy adopted under Subsection (a). The
  report must include an update on the implementation plan described
  by Subsection (b).
         SECTION 2.  (a) As soon as practicable after the effective
  date of this Act, the Texas Department of Criminal Justice shall
  adopt the standards required under Section 501.028, Government
  Code, as added by this Act.
         (b)  Not later than December 1, 2028, the Texas Department of
  Criminal Justice shall submit the first report required by Section
  501.028(c), Government Code, as added by this Act.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
