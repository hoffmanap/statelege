SENATE RESOLUTION NO. 40
         WHEREAS, Proud residents of Dallas are gathering in Austin
  on February 4, 2025, for Dallas Day at the State Capitol; and
 
         WHEREAS, Founded in 1841 and formally incorporated in
  1856, Dallas has grown into a thriving metropolis with a
  population of more than 1.3 million people; it serves as a hub for
  business, innovation, and culture, and its diverse economy
  includes finance, technology, health care, manufacturing, and
  transportation; and
 
         WHEREAS, A leader in corporate headquarters relocations,
  economic development, and job creation, Dallas is home to
  numerous Fortune 500 companies; the city's business-friendly
  environment, world-class infrastructure, and strategic location
  make it a powerhouse in the state, national, and global
  economies; and
 
         WHEREAS, Dallas offers premier shopping and dining
  experiences along with a vibrant arts community, which includes
  renowned cultural institutions such as the Dallas Museum of Art,
  the Morton H. Meyerson Symphony Center, the Nasher Sculpture
  Center, and the AT&T Performing Arts Center; in addition, the
  Dallas Arts District is the largest contiguous urban arts
  district in the nation; and
 
         WHEREAS, Dallas is a hub for sports and recreation; the
  city is home to the Dallas Mavericks and Dallas Stars, and
  residents are also devoted to the Dallas Cowboys, Texas Rangers,
  and FC Dallas, which play their home games in nearby cities; Fair
  Park, home to the historic Cotton Bowl Stadium and the annual
  State Fair of Texas, remains a cherished institution, drawing
  millions of visitors each year; and
 
         WHEREAS, The city offers exceptional green spaces and
  recreational opportunities, including Klyde Warren Park, White
  Rock Lake, and the Katy Trail, promoting a high quality of life
  for all those who live and work in the community; and
 
         WHEREAS, The University of Texas Southwestern Medical
  Center and Baylor University Medical Center stand as pillars of
  excellence in health care and medical research, providing
  world-class treatment and attracting top-tier medical
  professionals; and
 
         WHEREAS, Dallas continues to make significant strides in
  sustainability and innovation, with advancements in renewable
  energy, green building initiatives, and extensive public
  transportation options, including Dallas Area Rapid Transit
  (DART), one of the largest light rail networks in the nation; and
 
         WHEREAS, The city's leaders, in partnership with
  organizations such as the Dallas Regional Chamber, remain
  committed to economic growth, education, and community
  development, ensuring that Dallas remains one of the most vibrant
  and forward-thinking cities in the United States; and
 
         WHEREAS, With its bold spirit and dynamic vision, Dallas
  exemplifies the motto "Live large. Think big." and its citizens
  may take justifiable pride in the important role their community
  continues to play in the story of the Lone Star State; now,
  therefore, be it
 
         RESOLVED, That the Senate of the State of Texas, 89th
  Legislature hereby recognize February 4, 2025, as Dallas Day at
  the State Capitol and extend a warm welcome to the visiting
  delegation.
 
  Johnson, Hall, Parker, West
