By: Landgraf H.B. No. 854
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to state and local permitting and regulation of mobile
  food vendors; requiring an occupational license or permit;
  authorizing fees; providing for criminal and administrative
  penalties.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  This Act may be cited as the Lone Star License to
  Grill Act.
         SECTION 2.  Section 437B.001(2), Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
               (2)  "Food vending vehicle" means a motor vehicle,
  trailer, pushcart, or other [any] vehicle that operates as a food
  service establishment and is capable of being transported from one
  location to another, whether self-propelled or otherwise [designed
  to be readily movable]. The term includes a vehicle that:
                     (A)  remains at one location for an extended
  period; or
                     (B)  is connected while operating to an external
  water supply, wastewater disposal system, electrical source, or gas
  source, if each connection:
                           (i)  complies with applicable health and
  safety requirements governing the installation, use, and
  maintenance of the connection;
                           (ii)  can be disconnected; and
                           (iii)  does not prevent the vehicle from
  being transported without material structural alteration to the
  vehicle.
               The term does not include a building or other structure
  permanently affixed to real property in a manner that prevents the
  building or structure from being transported without material
  structural alteration. For purposes of Chapter 437, a food vending
  vehicle is considered a mobile food unit.
         SECTION 3.  Section 437B.003, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 437B.003.  LOCAL PREEMPTION; CERTAIN PROHIBITED
  REQUIREMENTS. (a) This chapter preempts a local authority's power
  to prohibit or regulate mobile food vendors in a manner that
  conflicts with this chapter.
         (b)  The department or a local authority may not, in
  administering or enforcing this chapter, Chapter 437, Chapter 437A,
  or another law for the public health regulation of a food vending
  vehicle's operation as a mobile food vendor, require a mobile food
  vendor to take an action that the executive commissioner is
  prohibited from requiring under Section 437B.004(4)(J), (K), or
  (L).
         SECTION 4.  Section 437B.004, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 437B.004.  RULES. The executive commissioner may adopt
  rules to implement this chapter. The rules must be narrowly
  tailored to address a demonstrable health or safety risk and may
  not:
               (1)  limit the number of mobile food vendor licenses
  the department may issue;
               (2)  address the hours of operation for mobile food
  vendors;
               (3)  restrict a mobile food vendor's propane capacity
  below the capacity state law allows for commercial vehicles; or
               (4)  require a mobile food vendor to:
                     (A)  operate outside a specific perimeter of a
  commercial establishment or restaurant;
                     (B)  enter into any agreement with a commercial
  establishment or restaurant, except as necessary to properly
  dispose of grease and other cooking waste;
                     (C)  have an operational handwashing sink in the
  vehicle of a vendor who sells only prepackaged food;
                     (D)  associate with a commissary if the vehicle
  carries the equipment necessary to comply with state law and
  properly disposes of grease and other cooking waste;
                     (E)  provide the vendor's fingerprints as a
  condition of holding a mobile food vendor license;
                     (F)  install a global positioning system tracking
  device on the vehicle;
                     (G)  keep the vehicle in constant motion except
  when serving customers;
                     (H)  submit to an additional fire inspection a
  vehicle the vendor demonstrates has passed a state or local fire
  inspection within the preceding 12 months; [or]
                     (I)  submit to a health inspection other than:
  [health inspections other than an inspection the department, or a
  local authority under a collaborative agreement, conducts unless
  the department is investigating a reported foodborne illness.]
                           (i)  an inspection authorized by Section
  437B.054 or Subchapter D and conducted by the department or a
  governmental entity acting under a collaborative agreement as
  provided by Subchapter D;
                           (ii)  an inspection under Chapter 437,
  Chapter 437A, or another law governing the public health regulation
  of the vehicle's operation as a mobile food vendor, if the
  inspection is conducted by the department or by the local authority
  that issued the applicable permit or with which an application for
  the applicable permit is pending, including an authorized agent or
  employee of the department or local authority; or
                           (iii)  an inspection conducted as part of an
  investigation authorized under Section 437B.201;
                     (J)  equip a food vending vehicle to operate
  independently of, demonstrate that the vehicle can operate
  independently of, or disconnect the vehicle while it is operating
  from, an external water supply, wastewater disposal system,
  electrical source, or gas source, if the connection complies with
  applicable health and safety requirements governing the
  installation, use, and maintenance of the connection;
                     (K)  remove or modify, or refrain from making or
  using, an alteration, attachment, addition, placement, or change
  in, under, around, or on a food vending vehicle solely because the
  alteration, attachment, addition, placement, or change reduces the
  vehicle's mobility, if the alteration, attachment, addition,
  placement, or change does not prevent the vehicle from being
  transported without material structural alteration to the vehicle;
  or
                     (L)  move a food vending vehicle at a specified
  interval or solely to demonstrate that the vehicle is movable,
  unless the department or a local authority authorized by law to
  inspect the vehicle has reasonable cause, based on specific facts,
  to believe the vehicle is permanently affixed to real property or is
  not capable of being transported without material structural
  alteration to the vehicle, in which case the department or local
  authority may, at a reasonable time, require the vehicle to be moved
  on one occasion based on those facts, solely to determine whether
  the vehicle is capable of being transported as required by Section
  437B.001(2).
         SECTION 5.  Section 437B.051, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 437B.051.  LICENSE OR PERMIT REQUIRED. (a) A person may
  not operate a food vending vehicle as a mobile food vendor in this
  state unless, for that vehicle, the person: [holds a mobile food
  vendor's license issued by the department.]
               (1)  holds a mobile food vendor license issued by the
  department under this chapter; or
               (2)  holds each permit required under Chapter 437 or
  another law for the public health regulation of the vehicle's
  operation as a mobile food vendor in each jurisdiction in which the
  vehicle operates.
         (b)  A separate license under this chapter is required for
  each food vending vehicle a mobile food vendor operates under a
  department-issued license.
         (c) [(b)]  A local authority may not prohibit the operation
  in its jurisdiction of a food vending vehicle for which a mobile
  food vendor holds a license issued under this chapter if the vendor
  [who holds a mobile food vendor license and] complies with all other
  state and local laws not in conflict with this chapter. With
  respect to that vehicle, a local authority may not require the
  vendor to:
               (1)  obtain a permit under Chapter 437 or another local
  permit, license, registration, certificate, or similar
  authorization imposed for the public health regulation of the
  vehicle's operation as a mobile food vendor;
               (2)  pay a fee imposed by or on behalf of the local
  authority for an authorization prohibited by Subdivision (1) or for
  an inspection of the vehicle or investigation of the vendor or
  vehicle, if the inspection or investigation concerns the public
  health regulation of the vehicle's operation as a mobile food
  vendor and is conducted under this chapter, Chapter 437, or another
  law;
               (3)  submit to a routine health inspection other than
  an inspection authorized by Section 437B.054 or Subchapter D; or
               (4)  comply, for the public health regulation of food
  preparation, handling, storage, or service in or from the vehicle,
  with a local operational or vehicle-equipment standard that is
  different from or in addition to a standard imposed by state law or
  a department rule. This subdivision does not apply to a requirement
  governing the installation, use, or maintenance of a connection
  described by Section 437B.001(2)(B).
         (d)  A local authority otherwise authorized by law to require
  a permit for the public health regulation of a food vending
  vehicle's operation as a mobile food vendor may require the vendor
  to obtain the permit and comply with its requirements for a food
  vending vehicle that is not operated under a license issued under
  this chapter. A permit described by Subsection (a)(2) authorizes
  operation only within the jurisdiction of the authority that issued
  the permit, except as otherwise provided by law.
         (e)  A person operating a food vending vehicle solely within
  the scope of a valid temporary food service establishment permit
  issued under Chapter 437 is not required to hold a license under
  this chapter. The department or a local authority may not deny a
  temporary food service establishment permit solely because the
  permitted operation will be conducted from a food vending vehicle
  or because the applicant is eligible to apply for a license under
  this chapter.
         (f)  In areas under the department's jurisdiction under
  Chapter 437, the department shall make available for an operation
  conducted from a food vending vehicle:
               (1)  a single-event temporary food service
  establishment permit for an event lasting not more than 14
  consecutive days; and
               (2)  a multiple-event temporary food service
  establishment permit valid for two years for an unlimited number of
  events, provided that each event lasts not more than 14 consecutive
  days.
         The aggregate amount of all fees charged by or on behalf of
  the department in connection with an application for, issuance or
  renewal of, or operation under a permit under Subdivision (1) may
  not exceed $52. The aggregate amount of all fees charged by or on
  behalf of the department in connection with an application for,
  issuance or renewal of, or operation under a permit under
  Subdivision (2) may not exceed $200. The applicable limit includes
  any application, permit, renewal, inspection, online transaction,
  technology, or processing fee, regardless of the entity that
  collects the fee on behalf of the department.
         (g)  A food vending vehicle operated under a license issued
  under this chapter is not subject to a permit or routine health
  inspection under Chapter 437 or another law for the public health
  regulation of the vehicle's operation as a mobile food vendor.
  Except as otherwise expressly provided by this chapter, the
  licensing, operational, and routine health inspection requirements
  of this chapter and rules adopted to implement those requirements
  apply only to a food vending vehicle operated under a license issued
  under this chapter. The permitting, routine health inspection, and
  operation of a food vending vehicle operated solely under a permit
  described by Subsection (a)(2), (e), or (f) are governed by Chapter
  437 and other applicable laws and rules. This subsection does not
  limit an investigation authorized under Section 437B.201 or an
  emergency action otherwise authorized by law.
         (h)  Notwithstanding Sections 12.0111 and 437.0125 or any
  other law, the aggregate amount of all fees charged by or on behalf
  of the department in connection with an initial application for or
  renewal of a permit required by Section 437.0055 for a food vending
  vehicle and the two-year permit term resulting from the application
  may not exceed $258 for each food vending vehicle. The applicable
  limit includes any application, permit, amendment, inspection,
  online transaction, technology, or processing fee, regardless of
  the entity that collects the fee on behalf of the department.
         (i)  In this section, "routine health inspection" means a
  periodic, scheduled, or randomized inspection conducted without
  reasonable suspicion of a violation and not in response to a
  complaint, reported foodborne illness, emergency, or need to verify
  correction of a documented violation identified during an
  inspection or investigation authorized by law.
         SECTION 6.  Section 437B.058, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended by adding Subsections (e) and (f) to read as follows:
         (e)  Notwithstanding any other law and except as provided by
  Subsection (f), the aggregate amount of all fees charged by or on
  behalf of the department in connection with an initial or renewal
  application and any license term resulting from the application may
  not exceed the sum of the applicable amount under Subdivision (1),
  (2), or (3) for each food vending vehicle included in the
  application:
               (1)  $300 for a mobile food type I vendor;
               (2)  $500 for a mobile food type II vendor; or
               (3)  $700 for a mobile food type III vendor.
         The applicable limit includes any application, license,
  vehicle substitution, amendment, inspection, online transaction,
  technology, or processing fee, regardless of the entity that
  collects the fee on behalf of the department.
         (f)  In addition to the amount authorized by Subsection (e),
  the department may assess a reinspection fee only if:
               (1)  the reinspection is reasonably necessary to verify
  correction of a documented violation that presents a direct and
  significant risk to public health;
               (2)  the fee does not exceed the lesser of the
  department's reasonable cost of conducting the reinspection or
  $200; and
               (3)  the department does not assess more than one
  reinspection fee for the food vending vehicle during any 60-day
  period, regardless of the number of violations for which correction
  is being verified, unless the license holder failed to make
  reasonable efforts to correct a violation for which the
  reinspection is conducted.
         The department may not charge a license holder a fee for
  conducting a complaint investigation or compliance inspection
  except for a reinspection authorized by this subsection.
         SECTION 7.  Section 437B.103, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 437B.103.  OPERATIONAL STANDARDS. A mobile food vendor
  shall:
               (1)  submit to and pass any health inspection required:
  [health inspection conducted under Subchapter D;]
                     (A)  under Subchapter D for a food vending vehicle
  operated under a license issued under this chapter; or
                     (B)  under Chapter 437, Chapter 437A, or another
  applicable law for a food vending vehicle operated under a permit
  described by Section 437B.051(a)(2), (e), or (f), as applicable;
  and
               (2)  display [the mobile food vendor license and health
  inspection certificate] in a conspicuous location for public view
  the license or permit authorizing the vendor's operation and any
  required health inspection certificate.
         SECTION 8.  Section 437.0055(a), Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         (a)  A person may not operate a food service establishment,
  retail food store, mobile food unit, or temporary food service
  establishment located in an area in which a county or public health
  district does not require a permit or conduct inspections under
  this chapter unless the person has a permit issued by the
  department. A permit is not required under this subsection for a
  mobile food unit that:
               (1)  is permitted and inspected by a municipality under
  authority granted by law and operates only within the jurisdiction
  of the municipality; or
               (2)  is operated by a person who holds a license for the
  unit under Chapter 437B.
         SECTION 9.  Section 437A.001(2), Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
               (2)  "Mobile food service establishment" means an
  operation under the managerial authority of a single person that
  consists of one or more mobile food units, roadside food vendors, or
  food vending vehicles [or roadside food vendors operating from a
  single preparation facility under the managerial authority of a
  single permit or license holder]. In this subdivision, "food
  vending vehicle" has the meaning assigned by Section 437B.001. The
  term includes an operation regardless of whether the operation uses
  a preparation facility or commissary. This subdivision does not
  affect a requirement imposed by other law concerning the use of a
  preparation facility or commissary.
         SECTION 10.  Chapter 437A, Health and Safety Code, is
  amended by adding Section 437A.003 to read as follows:
         Sec. 437A.003.  COUNTY PERMIT REQUIRED. (a) Notwithstanding
  any other law, a mobile food service establishment may not operate
  in a county to which this chapter applies unless each food vending
  vehicle, as that term is defined by Section 437B.001, that the
  establishment operates in the county is:
               (1)  covered by a permit issued to the establishment by
  the county;
               (2)  operated under a license issued by the department
  under Chapter 437B; or
               (3)  operated solely within the scope of a valid
  temporary food service establishment permit issued under Chapter
  437.
         (b)  A permit issued by a county under this chapter must
  identify each food vending vehicle covered by the permit and
  authorize the mobile food service establishment to operate each
  identified vehicle in any municipality located in the county.
  Notwithstanding any other law, a municipality or public health
  district with jurisdiction in the county may not require, in
  addition to a valid permit issued under this chapter, a permit,
  license, registration, certificate, or similar authorization for
  the public health regulation of the operation of a food vending
  vehicle identified in the permit.
         (c)  A permit issued by a county under this chapter is
  considered a permit issued by a county under Chapter 437 for
  purposes of that chapter.
         (d)  Except as authorized by Chapter 437B, a county's
  inspection and enforcement authority under this chapter applies
  only to a food vending vehicle covered or required to be covered by
  a permit issued by the county and does not apply to a food vending
  vehicle operated under a license issued under Chapter 437B.
         SECTION 11.  (a)  Not later than January 1, 2028, the
  executive commissioner of the Health and Human Services Commission
  shall adopt the rules necessary to implement the changes in law made
  by this Act, including rules necessary to:
               (1)  govern the department's issuance of permits under
  Section 437.0055, Health and Safety Code, for food vending
  vehicles;
               (2)  establish the minimum health and safety standards
  under Chapter 437, Health and Safety Code, applicable to food
  vending vehicles operated under permits described by Section
  437B.051(a)(2), Health and Safety Code, as amended by this Act; and
               (3)  conform the department's rules to the alternative
  licensing and permitting pathways established by Section 437B.051,
  Health and Safety Code, as amended by this Act.
         (b)  A mobile food vendor license issued under Chapter 437B,
  Health and Safety Code, before the effective date of this Act
  remains valid until the license expires or is suspended or revoked.
  Subsections (e) and (f), Section 437B.058, Health and Safety Code,
  as added by this Act, apply to a fee charged on or after the
  effective date of this Act. This Act does not require the refund of
  a fee paid before the effective date of this Act.
         (c)  A local authority may administer a mobile food unit
  permitting program authorized under Chapter 437 or 437A, Health and
  Safety Code, or another law, using an ordinance, order, rule,
  policy, or procedure adopted before, on, or after the effective
  date of this Act and not repealed or rescinded. A local authority
  is not required to readopt an ordinance, order, rule, policy, or
  procedure solely because the authority to administer the program
  was limited or preempted by Chapter 437B, Health and Safety Code, as
  that chapter existed before the effective date of this Act.
         (d)  Pending adoption of the rules required by Subsection (a)
  of this section, a rule governing mobile food vendors in effect
  immediately before the effective date of this Act remains in effect
  and may be enforced by the Department of State Health Services or a
  local authority, as applicable, except to the extent the rule
  conflicts with a change in law made by this Act. The department
  shall administer permits required by Section 437.0055, Health and
  Safety Code, including temporary food service establishment
  permits, for operations conducted from food vending vehicles in
  accordance with Section 437B.051, Health and Safety Code, as
  amended by this Act.
         (e)  Pending adoption of the rules required by Subsection (a)
  of this section, Sections 226.3 and 226.6, Title 25, Texas
  Administrative Code, as those sections existed immediately before
  the effective date of this Act, apply to a food vending vehicle
  operated solely under a permit described by Section 437B.051(a)(2),
  Health and Safety Code, as amended by this Act, except that Section
  226.6(b)(4), Title 25, Texas Administrative Code, does not apply
  and no other requirement applies to the extent the requirement
  conflicts with a change in law made by this Act. For purposes of
  applying those rules, a term defined by Chapter 437B, Health and
  Safety Code, has the meaning assigned by that chapter, as amended by
  this Act; a term, other than "regulatory authority," that is not
  defined by that chapter but is defined by Section 226.2, Title 25,
  Texas Administrative Code, has the meaning assigned by Section
  226.2 as that section existed immediately before the effective date
  of this Act; and "regulatory authority" means the department or
  local authority that issued the permit.
         SECTION 12.  This Act takes effect immediately if it
  receives a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each
  house, as provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.
  If this Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate
  effect, this Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
