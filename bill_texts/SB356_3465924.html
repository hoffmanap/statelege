A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the adoption of a water conservation program by a
  county.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 562, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Subchapter C to read as follows:
  SUBCHAPTER C. WATER CONSERVATION
         Sec. 562.051.  WATER CONSERVATION PROGRAMS. (a)  The
  commissioners court of a county by order may adopt a water
  conservation program.  Except as provided by Subsection (b), a
  water conservation program adopted under this section may establish
  for the unincorporated area of the county, including the
  extraterritorial jurisdiction of a municipality, only:
               (1)  water use restrictions;
               (2)  water conservation standards applicable to a
  development, redevelopment, or subdivision of a tract of land that
  begins after August 31, 2027; and
               (3)  other strategies intended to reduce water use or
  water loss or otherwise conserve water resources.
         (b)  Water use restrictions and water conservation standards
  established under Subsection (a) do not apply to an agricultural
  operation, as defined by Section 251.002, Agriculture Code.
         (c)  To the extent of a conflict between a county order
  adopted under this section and a permit, rule, ordinance, or other
  measure issued by a governmental entity and regulating the same
  conduct, the measure prevails.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
