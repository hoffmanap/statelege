A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the treatment of a manufactured home as real property.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 1201.2055(a), Occupations Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
         (a)  In completing an application for the issuance of a
  statement of ownership, an owner of a manufactured home shall
  indicate whether the owner elects to treat the home as real
  property.  An owner may elect to treat a manufactured home as real
  property only if the home is:
               (1)  attached to:
                     (A) [(1)]  real property that is owned by the
  owner of the home; or
                     (B) [(2)]  land leased to the owner of the home
  under a long-term lease, as defined by department rule; or
               (2)  occupied by the owner of the home as the owner's
  primary residence.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
