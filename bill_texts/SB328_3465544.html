  By: Huffman S.B. No. 328
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the operation near a public or private primary or
  secondary school or a preschool of a retail establishment selling
  vaping apparatuses or tobacco products; providing a civil penalty.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter H, Chapter 161, Health and Safety
  Code, is amended by adding Section 161.0877 to read as follows:
         Sec. 161.0877.  RETAIL SALE OF VAPING APPARATUS OR TOBACCO
  PRODUCT NEAR SCHOOL; CIVIL PENALTY. (a) In this section:
               (1)  "School" means a public or private primary or
  secondary school or a preschool, as defined by Section 36.003.
               (2)  "Vaping apparatus" means a battery-operated or
  electronic device intended to deliver an aerosolized or vaporized
  substance to the individual inhaling from the device, including:
                     (A)  an e-cigar, e-pipe, vape pen, or e-hookah;
                     (B)  any component, part, or accessory of the
  device; and
                     (C)  any liquid, solution, or substance intended
  to be aerosolized or vaporized by the device, regardless of whether
  the liquid, solution, or substance includes nicotine, flavoring, or
  other chemicals.
         (b)  This section applies only to a retail establishment for
  which at least 50 percent of the total gross receipts from retail
  sales on the establishment's premises is derived from the retail
  sale of cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vaping apparatuses, and tobacco
  products.
         (c)  A retailer may not operate a retail establishment that
  sells vaping apparatuses or tobacco products within 1,000 feet of a
  school.
         (d)  A retailer who violates Subsection (c) is subject to
  civil penalty in an amount of not less than $500 and not more than
  $2,000 per day the violation continues.
         (e)  The attorney general may bring an action to collect a
  civil penalty imposed under this section. Civil penalties shall be
  deposited in the general revenue fund.  The attorney general may
  recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs in bringing an action
  under this section.
         (f)  The distance between a retail establishment and a school
  is measured:
               (1)  in a direct line from the property line of the
  school to the property line of the establishment and in a direct
  line across intersections; or
               (2)  for an establishment located on or above the fifth
  floor of a multistory building, in a direct line from the property
  line of the school to the property line of the establishment, in a
  direct line across intersections, and in a vertical line from the
  building base at the property line to the base of the floor on which
  the establishment is located.
         SECTION 2.  Notwithstanding Section 161.0877, Health and
  Safety Code, as added by this Act, a retailer may continue to
  operate a retail establishment that begins to operate before the
  effective date of this Act and that sells vaping apparatuses or
  tobacco products within 1,000 feet of a school, as defined by that
  section, until September 1, 2029.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
