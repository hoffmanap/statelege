A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the authority of a municipality or county to prohibit
  the operation or construction of certain commercial and industrial
  facilities located near a residential property.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 250, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 250.015 to read as follows:
         Sec. 250.015.  REGULATION OF COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL
  FACILITIES NEAR RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Commercial facility" means an improvement, other
  than an industrial facility, operated or constructed primarily for
  the purpose of engaging in commerce.
               (2)  "Improvement" and "tangible personal property"
  have the meanings assigned by Section 1.04, Tax Code.
               (3)  "Industrial facility" means an improvement
  operated or constructed primarily for the purpose of:
                     (A)  manufacturing or processing tangible
  personal property to be sold at retail or wholesale, or to be
  incorporated into other tangible personal property to be sold at
  retail or wholesale; or
                     (B)  housing servers and related equipment and
  support staff for the processing, storage, and distribution of
  data.
               (4)  "Sport shooting range" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 250.001.
         (b)  This section does not apply to a commercial or
  industrial facility that:
               (1)  operates or is proposed to be operated only
  between the hours of 8 a.m. and 5 p.m.;
               (2)  is required to be operated or constructed by state
  or federal law;
               (3)  is used for less than 90 days as part of the
  development of land, construction of an improvement to land, or
  exploration or development of oil or gas; or
               (4)  is a sport shooting range.
         (c)  A municipality or county by ordinance, order, or other
  measure may prohibit the operation or construction of a commercial
  or industrial facility that:
               (1)  is or is proposed to be located less than 1,000
  feet from a residential improvement used as the temporary or
  permanent home of one or more individuals, including a
  single-family dwelling or a unit in a multi-unit building such as an
  apartment or condominium; and
               (2)  when operating generates or will generate for more
  than 10 seconds at a time, measured at the property line of the
  nearest residential improvement:
                     (A)  a sound level that exceeds 50 dBA or 50 dBC;
                     (B)  a vibration frequency that exceeds one hertz;
  or
                     (C)  a brightness intensity that exceeds five
  foot-candles.
         (d)  This section does not authorize a county to regulate a
  commercial or industrial facility unless the facility is located in
  the unincorporated area of the county.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
