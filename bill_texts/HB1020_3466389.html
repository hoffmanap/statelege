By: Perez of Harris H.B. No. 1020
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to criminal offenses involving motor fuel metering devices
  and fuel theft and to the Financial Crimes Intelligence Center;
  creating criminal offenses.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 16, Penal Code, is amended by adding
  Section 16.021 to read as follows:
         Sec. 16.021.  INTERFERENCE WITH MOTOR FUEL METERING DEVICE.  
  (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Authorized representative" means a person
  authorized by a dealer to act on the dealer's behalf for the purpose
  of performing the dealer's duties or obligations under this chapter
  with respect to a motor fuel facility, motor fuel dispenser, or
  motor fuel metering device.
               (2)  "Dealer," has the meanings assigned by Section
  2310.001, Occupations Code.
               (3)  "Diesel exhaust fluid," "dispenser," "dispenser
  manipulation device," "liquid measuring device," and "skimmer"
  have the meaning assigned by Section 426.001, Government Code.
               (4)  "Fuel" means gasoline or diesel fuel, as those
  terms are defined by Section 162.001, Tax Code.
               (5)  "Service Technician" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 2310.151, Occupations Code.
         (b)  A person commits an offense if the person:
               (1)  knowingly intercepts, disrupts, or otherwise
  interferes with, or attempts to intercept, disrupt, or otherwise
  interfere with the operation of a dispenser or liquid measuring
  device;
               (2)  knowingly modifies or attempts to modify a liquid
  measuring device;
               (3)  knowingly manufactures, assembles, possesses,
  sells, or attempts to sell a dispenser manipulation device;
               (4)  knowingly installs, attaches, connects, or
  attempts to install, attach, or connect a dispenser manipulation
  device on or in a dispenser or liquid measuring device;
               (5)  knowingly receives or accepts, directly or
  indirectly, a financial or material benefit knowing that the
  benefit is derived from conduct described by Subdivisions (1)-(4)
  of this subsection.
         (c)  It is an exception to the application of this section
  that:
               (1)  the actor is:
                     (A)  an employee or authorized representative of
  the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation;
                     (B)  a service technician;
                     (C)  a dealer, or an employee, contractor, or
  authorized representative of a dealer; or
                     (D)  a person acting at the request of, or under an
  agreement with, a law enforcement agency or the Texas Department of
  Licensing and Regulation; and
               (2)  acts within the course and scope of the actor's
  employment, agency, contract, or official duties to:
                     (A)  inspect, test, calibrate, maintain, repair,
  replace, or otherwise service a dispenser or liquid measuring
  device in accordance with applicable law and manufacturer
  specifications;
                     (B)  identify, inspect, disable, remove,
  document, secure, preserve, test, possess, transport, transfer, or
  store a dispenser manipulation device;
                     (C)  deliver a dispenser manipulation device to a
  law enforcement agency or another governmental authority
  authorized to investigate an offense under this section; or
                     (D)  conduct training exercises in accordance
  with Subsection (d).
         (d)  A person may possess a dispenser manipulation device for
  the purpose of conducting training for law enforcement officers,
  Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation employees, service
  technicians, dealers, or employees or authorized representatives
  of a dealer to provide instruction on how to:
               (1)  identify, inspect, disable, remove, document,
  secure, preserve, test, possess, transport, transfer, or store a
  dispenser manipulation device; or
               (2)  deliver a dispenser manipulation device to a law
  enforcement agency or another governmental authority authorized to
  investigate an offense involving the device.
         (e)  A dispenser manipulation device utilized for training
  exercises as described in Subsection (d) must be:
               (1)  rendered incapable of manipulating, altering, or
  interfering with the operation or measurement of a dispenser or
  liquid measuring device; and
               (2)  obtained from or with the authorization of the
  Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, a law enforcement
  agency, or as otherwise provided by a rule adopted by the Texas
  Commission of Licensing and Regulation.
         (f)  It is an exception to the application of this section
  that the actor is:
               (1)  a peace officer, federal special investigator,
  attorney representing the state, or a federal prosecutor acting
  within the course and scope of the actor's official duties;
               (2)  acting at the direction of a person described by
  Subdivision (1) in connection with the investigation or prosecution
  of an offense involving a skimmer or dispenser manipulation device;
  or
               (3)  acting under an order of a court.
         (g)  An exception provided by this section does not apply to
  an actor who is acting outside the course and scope of the actor's
  employment or official duties or who intends to commit, facilitate,
  conceal, or profit from an offense.
         (h)  An offense under this section is a felony of the second
  degree.

         SECTION 3.  Article 42.019(a), Code of Criminal Procedure,
  is amended to read as follows:
         (a)  A judge shall enter an affirmative finding in the
  judgment in a case if the judge or jury, whichever is the finder of
  fact, determines beyond a reasonable doubt in the guilt or
  innocence phase of the trial of an offense under Section 31.03 or
  Section 16.021, Penal Code, that the defendant, in committing the
  offense:
               (1)  dispensed, pumped, siphoned, or otherwise removed
  [motor] fuel from a dispenser, storage tank, or associated piping
  [into the fuel tank of a motor vehicle] on the premises of an
  establishment at which [motor] fuel is offered for retail sale; and
               (2)  after dispensing, pumping, siphoning, or
  otherwise removing the [motor] fuel, left the premises of the
  establishment without paying the establishment the full amount owed
  for the motor fuel.
         (c)  In this article, "fuel" means "gasoline" or "diesel
  fuel," as those terms are defined by Section 162.001, Tax Code.
         SECTION 4.  Section 426.001, Government Code, as added by
  Ch. 542 (H.B. 201), Acts 2025 of the 89th Legislature, Regular
  Session, 2025, is amended to read as follows:
         Sec. 426.001.  DEFINITIONS. In this chapter:
         (1)  "Card fraud" means an act that constitutes the offense
  of fraudulent use or possession of a credit card or debit card under
  Section 32.315, Penal Code.
         (2)  "Center" means the financial crimes intelligence center
  established under this chapter.
         (2-a)  "Check fraud" means conduct that constitutes an
  offense under Section 32.21, Penal Code, with respect to a sight
  order, as defined by Section 1.07, Penal Code.
         (3)  "Commission" means the Texas Commission of Licensing
  and Regulation.
         (4)  "Department" means the Texas Department of Licensing
  and Regulation.
         (4-X)  "Diesel exhaust fluid" means an aqueous solution of
  urea used as a liquid reducing agent (other than fuel) in
  conjunction with selective catalytic reduction to reduce NOX
  emissions sold at retail through a dispenser at a motor fuel
  facility for delivery directly into a vehicle or a portable
  container.  The term does not include diesel exhaust fluid sold in a
  prepackaged container.
         (4-X)  "Dispenser" means equipment designed to measure and
  deliver motor fuel or diesel exhaust fluid, including the housing
  and internal components of the equipment.
         (4-a)  "Electronic fund transfer" means any transfer of
  funds, other than a transaction originated by check, money order,
  or similar paper instrument, that is initiated through an
  electronic terminal, telephonic instrument, computer, or magnetic
  tape and that orders, instructs, or authorizes a financial
  institution to debit or credit an account.
         (4-X)  "Fuel" means "gasoline" or "diesel fuel," as those
  terms are defined by Section 162.001, Tax Code.
         (4-X)  "Liquid measuring device" means a mechanical or
  electronic device used to measure, determine, indicate, or record
  the quantity of fuel or diesel exhaust fluid dispensed or
  delivered.  The term includes a meter, pulser, encoder, and
  associated wiring, electronics, and indicating elements used to
  generate, transmit, or process a signal relating to that quantity.  
  The term does not include an unattended payment terminal or
  equipment used solely for payment processing.
         (4-a)  "Dispenser [Motor fuel] manipulation device" means a
  mechanism instrument, electronic component, or software
  manufactured, assembled, modified, programmed, or adapted for the
  purpose of manipulating, bypassing, overriding, or altering [to
  manipulate or alter] a dispenser or liquid measuring [motor fuel
  metering] device to cause [or a motor fuel unattended payment
  terminal for an unlawful purpose.]:
               (A)  fuel or diesel exhaust fluid to be dispensed
  without authorization or required payment;
               (B)  the quantity of fuel or diesel exhaust fluid
  dispensed to be inaccurately measured, displayed, recorded, or
  accounted for; or
               (C)  the amount charged or paid for fuel or diesel
  exhaust fluid to be inaccurately calculated or recorded.
         (4-b)  "Payment fraud" means:
                     (A)  conduct constituting card fraud or check
  fraud;
                     (B)  sending an unauthorized payment order;
                     (C)  initiating an electronic fund transfer
  without the consent of the account holder; or
                     (D)  any other act involving a fraudulent order
  for payment of money, as defined by commission rule.
         (4-b)  "Fuel [Motor fuel] theft" means an act that:
                     (A)  constitutes an offense under Section 31.03 or
  Section 16.021, Penal Code; and
                     (B)  qualifies for an affirmative finding of motor
  fuel theft under Article 42.019, Code of Criminal Procedure.
         (5)  "Skimmer" means an electronic, mechanical, or other
  device that may be used to unlawfully intercept electronic
  communications or data to perpetrate card fraud. The term includes
  a credit card shimmer.
         (6)  "Unauthorized payment order" means a payment order
  received by a receiving bank that is not:
                     (A)  an authorized order of a customer identified
  as sender under Section 4A.202(a), Business & Commerce Code; or
                     (B)  effective as an order of the customer under
  Section 4A.202(b) of that code.
         SECTION 5.  Section 426.054, Government Code, is amended to
  read as follows:
         (a)  The center may collaborate with federal, state, and
  local governmental agencies to accomplish the purposes of the
  center.
         (b)  The center shall assist law enforcement agencies, other
  governmental agencies, financial institutions, credit card
  issuers, debit card issuers, payment card networks, [institutions
  of higher education,] and merchants in their efforts to develop and
  implement strategies to:
         (1)  prevent and respond to payment fraud;
         (2)  detect skimmers; and
         (3)  ensure an effective response if a skimmer is found.
         (b)  The center shall assist law enforcement agencies, other
  governmental agencies, financial institutions, credit card
  issuers, debit card issuers, payment card networks, [institutions
  of higher education,] and merchants in their efforts to develop and
  implement strategies to:
               (1)  detect skimmers and dispenser [motor fuel]
  manipulation devices in or on a dispenser or liquid measuring
  device;
               (2)  ensure an effective response if a skimmer or
  dispenser [motor fuel] manipulation device is found in or on a
  dispenser or liquid measuring device; [and]
               (3)  prevent card fraud, and [motor] fuel theft.
               (b-1)  On request of a law enforcement or other
  governmental agency, the center may provide assistance to that
  agency regarding any matter within the center's expertise.
         (c)  The center may:
               (1)  serve as a centralized collection point for
  information related to payment fraud and [motor] fuel theft;
               (2)  provide training and educational opportunities to
  law enforcement;
               (3)  provide outreach to the public; and
               (4)  release information to affected financial
  institutions, credit card issuers, debit card issuers, payment card
  networks, [institutions of higher education,]and merchants if the
  center does not consider the information to be sensitive to law
  enforcement.
         (d)  For purposes of Subsection (c)(4), information is
  considered sensitive to law enforcement if the information could
  cause harm to law enforcement activities or jeopardize an
  investigation or operation if disclosed.
