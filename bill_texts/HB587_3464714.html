A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the authority of a county to regulate the construction
  of certain industrial and offensive land use facilities.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 240, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Subchapter F to read as follows:
  SUBCHAPTER F. REGULATION OF CERTAIN INDUSTRIAL AND OFFENSIVE LAND
  USE FACILITIES
         Sec. 240.101.  DEFINITIONS.  In this subchapter:
               (1)  "Alternative energy facility" means:
                     (A)  a wind power facility as defined by Section
  301.0001, Utilities Code;
                     (B)  a solar power facility as defined by Section
  302.0001, Utilities Code; or
                     (C)  a battery storage facility.
               (2)  "Industrial facility" includes:
                     (A)  an electric generation facility;
                     (B)  an alternative energy facility;
                     (C)  a manufacturing or production facility;
                     (D)  a distribution or logistics center;
                     (E)  a data center;
                     (F)  a water or wastewater treatment plant; and
                     (G)  another heavy industrial or large-scale
  commercial facility.
               (3)  "Offensive land use facility" includes a concrete
  plant, concrete crushing facility, landfill, solid waste transfer
  station, or automotive wrecking and salvage yard.
         Sec. 240.102.  PERMIT REQUIRED. Notwithstanding any other
  law, a person may not construct an industrial facility or offensive
  land use facility in the unincorporated area of a county unless the
  person obtains a permit from the commissioners court of the county.
         Sec. 240.103.  PERMITTING PROCEDURE. (a) A commissioners
  court of a county shall by order establish a procedure to review and
  approve an application for a permit to construct an industrial
  facility or offensive land use facility.
         (b)  A permitting procedure established under this section
  must require:
               (1)  a permit be issued by order of the commissioners
  court; and
               (2)  the commissioners court to hold at least two
  public hearings on the application for the permit not less than two
  weeks and not more than two months apart.
         Sec. 240.104.  DETERMINATION OF INDUSTRIAL OR OFFENSIVE LAND
  USE FACILITY. A commissioners court by order may determine that a
  facility proposed to be constructed is:
               (1)  an offensive land use facility; or
               (2)  an industrial facility, if the commissioners court
  by order determines that the proposed facility:
                     (A)  will require substantial infrastructure or
  resources when operational; or
                     (B)  by its nature, scale, or type of operations
  constitutes a facility that would be suited for location in a
  municipal zoning district that allows industrial use, if the
  project were proposed to be located in an area subject to municipal
  zoning regulation.
         Sec. 240.105.  ISSUANCE OF PERMIT. (a) In issuing a permit
  required by this subchapter, a commissioners court must consider:
               (1)  the compatibility of the proposed facility that is
  the subject of the permit with existing land uses in the surrounding
  area;
               (2)  the potential impact of the facility on:
                     (A)  public health and safety;
                     (B)  air, water, and soil quality; and
                     (C)  local infrastructure, including roads,
  utilities, and emergency services;
               (3)  public comment and input from county residents and
  other stakeholders; and
               (4)  other factors that the commissioners court
  determines are relevant to balancing the interest in development
  and the welfare of the community.
         (b)  A permit issued under this section may include
  conditions and requirements that the commissioners court
  determines are necessary to mitigate potential negative effects of
  the proposed facility that is the subject of the permit, including a
  requirement that the applicant implement measures for:
               (1)  the protection of public health and safety,
  including providing necessary training and equipment for emergency
  services;
               (2)  the improvement or maintenance of local
  infrastructure;
               (3)  the protection of air, water, and soil quality;
  and
               (4)  noise reduction.
         Sec. 240.106.  REVOCATION OR SUSPENSION OF PERMIT. A
  commissioners court may revoke or suspend a permit issued under
  this subchapter if the commissioners court by order finds that:
               (1)  the permit holder has violated the terms of the
  permit; or
               (2)  the permitted project causes undue harm to the
  surrounding area or community.
         Sec. 240.107.  ENFORCEMENT. The commissioners court of a
  county may adopt orders to enforce this subchapter or an order
  adopted under this subchapter.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
