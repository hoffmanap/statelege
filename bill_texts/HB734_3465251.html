By: Perez of El Paso H.B. No. 734
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the public disclosure of local economic development
  agreements involving data centers.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 403.0246, Government Code, is amended by
  adding Subsections (c-1) and (c-2) to read as follows:
         (c-1)  In this subsection and Subsection (c-2):
               (1)  "Data center" means any facility that primarily
  contains electronic equipment used to process, store, or transmit
  digital information, including a facility used primarily for
  cryptocurrency mining or other distributed ledger or blockchain
  computation and a facility used primarily for the training or
  operation of an artificial intelligence model.  The term does not
  include:
                     (A)  a facility owned and operated by this state,
  a political subdivision of this state, or an institution of higher
  education as defined by Section 61.003, Education Code;
                     (B)  electronic equipment, or the space
  containing electronic equipment, that supports a manufacturing,
  health care, financial, retail, or other business operation
  conducted by the owner or operator of the equipment at the same
  site, if housing that equipment is not the primary use of the site;
  or
                     (C)  a facility used primarily by a
  telecommunications provider to place tangible personal property
  that is used to deliver telecommunications services.
               (2)  "Data center agreement" means a local development
  agreement under which a tax abatement, loan, grant, rebate, refund,
  or other payment or benefit is made, computed, or conditioned
  wholly or partly on the construction, expansion, operation, or
  location of a data center, or on the amount of taxes or fees paid by
  or attributable to a data center or the owner or operator of a data
  center.
         (c-2)  Notwithstanding Chapter 552 or any other law, the
  electronic copy of a data center agreement required by Subsection
  (c)(8) must be a complete copy of the agreement, including each
  schedule, exhibit, and amendment, without redaction, except for
  information that the attorney general has determined under
  Subchapter G, Chapter 552, to be excepted from required public
  disclosure under Section 552.110.  The local government shall
  submit with the agreement a copy of each attorney general decision
  on which a redaction relies.
         SECTION 2.  Section 403.0247, Government Code, is amended by
  amending Subsection (e) and adding Subsection (f) to read as
  follows:
         (e)  It is a defense to an action brought under this section
  that the local government provided the required information or
  documents to the extent the information or documents are not exempt
  from disclosure or confidential under Chapter 552, except that for
  a data center agreement, as defined by Section 403.0246(c-1), it is
  a defense only that the local government provided the information
  and documents required by Section 403.0246(c-2).
         (f)  A local government that has not complied with Section
  403.0246(c-2) with respect to a data center agreement may not make a
  payment or provide an abatement or other benefit under the
  agreement until the local government complies.  A payment or
  benefit that would have accrued under the agreement during the
  period of noncompliance is forfeited.
         SECTION 3.  The changes in law made by this Act apply only to
  a local development agreement entered into on or after the
  effective date of this Act.  For purposes of this section, the
  renewal or extension of an agreement on or after the effective date
  of this Act, or an amendment of an agreement on or after that date
  that increases the amount or extends the duration of a benefit
  provided under the agreement, is considered the entering into of a
  new agreement on the date of the renewal, extension, or amendment.
         SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
