A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to a permit issued by the Parks and Wildlife Department for
  possessing a raccoon as a pet; authorizing a fee; providing an
  administrative penalty.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 71.004, Parks and Wildlife Code, is
  amended by adding Subsection (b) to read as follows:
         (b)  Subsection (a) does not apply to the possession of a
  raccoon by the holder of a permit issued under Chapter 72.
         SECTION 2.  Subtitle C, Title 5, Parks and Wildlife Code, is
  amended by adding Chapter 72 to read as follows:
  CHAPTER 72.  PET RACCOON PERMIT
  SUBCHAPTER A.  GENERAL PROVISIONS
         Sec. 72.001.  DEFINITION. In this chapter, "raccoon" means
  the species Procyon lotor.
         Sec. 72.002.  COMMISSION RULES. The commission shall adopt
  rules as necessary to administer this chapter.
         Sec. 72.003.  LIMITATION ON MUNICIPAL OR COUNTY REGULATION.
  Notwithstanding any other law, a municipality or county may not
  adopt or enforce an ordinance, order, or other regulation that
  conflicts with or is more stringent than this chapter or a
  commission rule adopted under this chapter.
         Sec. 72.004.  RACCOON OWNERSHIP AWARENESS PROGRAM. The
  department shall implement a raccoon ownership awareness program as
  prescribed by commission rule to inform the public of the
  requirements for raccoon ownership in this state.
  SUBCHAPTER B.  PET RACCOON PERMIT
         Sec. 72.051.  PERMIT REQUIRED. A person may not possess a
  raccoon as a pet unless the person holds a permit issued under this
  chapter.
         Sec. 72.052.  PERMIT APPLICATION; FEE. (a) An applicant for
  a permit under this chapter must apply to the department in the
  manner prescribed by commission rule.
         (b)  The permit application must be accompanied by an
  application fee in the amount set by the commission by rule.
         Sec. 72.053.  PERMIT ISSUANCE. The department shall issue a
  permit under this chapter to a person who meets the requirements of
  this chapter and commission rules adopted under this chapter.
         Sec. 72.054.  RACCOON OWNERSHIP COURSE REQUIRED. An
  applicant for a permit under this chapter must have completed a
  raccoon ownership course approved by the commission by rule.
         Sec. 72.055.  PERMIT TERM. A permit issued under this
  chapter is valid for one year.
         Sec. 72.056.  PERMIT RENEWAL. The holder of a permit issued
  under this chapter may apply to the department to renew the permit
  as prescribed by commission rule.
  SUBCHAPTER C.  RESPONSIBILITIES OF PERMIT HOLDER
         Sec. 72.101.  SOURCE OF RACCOON POSSESSED UNDER PERMIT. (a)
  A permit issued under this chapter does not authorize the permit
  holder to take a raccoon.
         (b)  A raccoon possessed under this chapter may be acquired
  only:
               (1)  as prescribed by commission rule; and
               (2)  from:
                     (A)  a person authorized to rescue or rehabilitate
  raccoons in this state;
                     (B)  a person licensed by the United States
  Department of Agriculture to breed raccoons; or
                     (C)  another holder of a permit issued under this
  chapter.
         Sec. 72.102.  REQUIRED VETERINARY CARE; VACCINES.  The
  holder of a permit issued under this chapter shall arrange for the
  provision of appropriate veterinary care for a raccoon possessed
  under the permit as prescribed by commission rule, including
  administration to the raccoon of vaccines for rabies and other
  communicable diseases as required by commission rule.
         Sec. 72.103.  SHELTER, FOOD AND WATER, AND ENRICHMENT.  The
  holder of a permit issued under this chapter shall provide a raccoon
  possessed under the permit shelter, food and water, and enrichment
  suitable for the raccoon as prescribed by commission rule.
         Sec. 72.104.  BREEDING PROHIBITED. The holder of a permit
  issued under this chapter may not breed a raccoon possessed under
  the permit unless the person is otherwise authorized to breed
  raccoons by the United Stated Department of Agriculture.
  SUBCHAPTER D. ENFORCEMENT
         Sec. 72.151.  INSPECTIONS. (a)  A game warden or other
  authorized department employee may inspect as prescribed by
  commission rule:
               (1)  a premises where the holder of a permit issued
  under this chapter possesses a raccoon; and
               (2)  records the holder of a permit issued under this
  chapter is required by commission rule to maintain regarding a
  raccoon possessed under this chapter.
         (b)  A commission rule adopted under this chapter regarding
  the inspection of the premises or records of the holder of a permit
  issued under this chapter may not authorize inspection by the
  department more than once per year except for an inspection in
  response to a violation, alleged violation, or complaint received
  regarding a violation or alleged violation of this chapter or a
  commission rule adopted under this chapter.
         Sec. 72.152.  SEIZURE OF RACCOON; TRANSFER OR DISPOSITION.  
  (a)  A peace officer or an authorized department employee may seize
  a raccoon possessed under a permit issued under this chapter if the
  officer or employee has probable cause to believe the permit holder
  is violating this chapter or a commission rule adopted under this
  chapter.
         (b)  The department may transfer a raccoon seized under this
  section to the possession of:
               (1)  another holder of a permit issued under this
  chapter; or
               (2)  a person authorized to rescue or rehabilitate
  raccoons in this state.
         (c)  The department may destroy a raccoon seized under this
  section that is determined to be dangerous or harmful as prescribed
  by commission rule.
         (d)  If a raccoon is seized under this section, the
  department may assess against the holder of a permit issued under
  this chapter an administrative penalty in an amount reasonable to
  recover the costs of administering this section, not to exceed
  $1,000.
         SECTION 3.  As soon as practicable after the effective date
  of this Act, the Parks and Wildlife Commission shall adopt rules
  necessary to implement Chapter 72, Parks and Wildlife Code, as
  added by this Act.
         SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
