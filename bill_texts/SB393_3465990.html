A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the location of certain cement kilns authorized by an
  air quality permit.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter C, Chapter 382, Health and Safety
  Code, is amended by adding Section 382.0651 to read as follows:
         Sec. 382.0651.  CERTAIN LOCATIONS FOR CEMENT KILNS
  PROHIBITED. (a)  The commission may not issue a permit under
  Section 382.0518 to construct a facility that is a cement kiln
  within 440 yards of a building in use as a school or place of worship
  at the time the application for the permit is filed.
         (b)  The commission may not issue a federal operating permit
  for a federal source that is a cement kiln classified as "major"
  under any applicable permitting requirements of the federal Clean
  Air Act (42 U.S.C. Section 7401 et seq.), or regulations adopted
  under that Act, within 440 yards of a building in use as a school or
  place of worship at the time the application for the permit is
  filed. 
         (c)  The measurement of distance for the purposes of this
  section is the shortest distance between the facility or source and
  the building in use as a school or place of worship.
         SECTION 2.  Section 382.0651, Health and Safety Code, as
  added by this Act, applies only to a permit issued by the Texas
  Commission on Environmental Quality on or after the effective date
  of this Act.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
