By: Mizani H.B. No. 1075
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to an exemption of residence homesteads from school
  district maintenance and operations ad valorem taxation and state
  aid to replace the resulting loss of school district operating
  revenue.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter F, Chapter 48, Education Code, is
  amended by adding Section 48.2544 to read as follows:
         Sec. 48.2544.  STATE AID FOR RESIDENCE HOMESTEAD OPERATING
  TAX EXEMPTION. (a) Beginning with the 2028-2029 school year, a
  school district, including a district otherwise ineligible for
  state aid, is entitled to state aid for the maintenance and
  operations tax revenue it loses solely because of the exemption
  under Section 11.13(s), Tax Code. The aid for each school year is
  based on the tax year beginning in the calendar year in which that
  school year begins.
         (b)  The commissioner shall compare the district's
  maintenance and operations tax levy with and without the exemption,
  accounting for the district's adopted rates, other applicable
  exemptions, tax ceilings, part-year exemptions, and tax roll
  corrections. The commissioner shall adjust the difference to
  reflect the district's expected collection rate. The assessor for
  the district shall certify and update the information needed for
  the calculation as prescribed by commissioner rule.
         (c)  Except for aid under this section, the commissioner
  shall calculate state aid, local share, local tax effort, excess
  local revenue, and the maximum compressed tax rate under Chapters
  46, 48, and 49 as if the exemption were not in effect. The
  comptroller shall determine and certify taxable property values
  under Section 403.302, Government Code, on the same basis. The
  chief appraiser and assessor for the district shall provide the
  information needed for those calculations.
         (d)  The commissioner shall pay the aid on the district's
  Foundation School Program payment schedule from general revenue
  appropriated for this purpose. The commissioner may make estimated
  payments and shall reconcile those payments with the district's
  entitlement. An overallocation is subject to recovery under
  Section 48.272.
         (e)  For purposes of Chapter 311, Tax Code, the portion of
  aid under this section attributable to property in a reinvestment
  zone is treated as school district tax increment only to the extent
  necessary to satisfy the district's obligation under an agreement
  executed before January 1, 2028, to pay tax increment into the tax
  increment fund for the zone.
         (f)  The commissioner and the comptroller shall adopt rules
  as necessary to administer this section.
         SECTION 2.  Section 11.13, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Subsections (s) and (t) to read as follows:
         (s)  An adult entitled to an exemption under Subsection (b)
  is entitled to an exemption from ad valorem taxation by a school
  district for maintenance and operations purposes of the portion of
  the appraised value of the adult's residence homestead that remains
  taxable for those purposes after other applicable exemptions are
  applied. The exemption applies to taxes levied at the district's
  tier one maintenance and operations tax rate and enrichment tax
  rate under Section 45.0032, Education Code. The chief appraiser
  shall grant the exemption without a separate application to an
  adult granted the exemption under Subsection (b). This subsection
  is subject to Section 1-b(s), Article VIII, Texas Constitution,
  regarding a levy necessary to avoid impairment of a debt contract.
         (t)  For purposes of calculating the no-new-revenue tax
  rate, no-new-revenue maintenance and operations rate, and
  voter-approval tax rate of a school district under Chapter 26, the
  district shall use the taxable values and amounts of taxes that
  would apply if Subsection (s) were not in effect.
         SECTION 3.  Section 11.26, Tax Code, is amended by adding
  Subsection (q) to read as follows:
         (q)  The chief appraiser and assessor shall determine and
  maintain the limitation on tax increases and the amounts necessary
  to administer and transfer the limitation as if the exemption under
  Section 11.13(s) were not in effect. For a residence homestead
  subject to the limitation, the assessor shall determine the debt
  service tax due by apportioning the amount of school district tax
  that would be imposed after application of the limitation and in the
  absence of the exemption between maintenance and operations and
  debt service in proportion to the district's adopted rates for
  those purposes. The amount apportioned to debt service is the debt
  service tax due. The assessor shall apply Section 26.1115
  separately to each component for a homestead that qualifies for the
  exemption for a portion of the tax year.
         SECTION 4.  The changes in law made by this Act apply only to
  ad valorem taxes imposed for a tax year beginning on or after
  January 1, 2028, and to state aid for a school year beginning with
  the 2028-2029 school year.
         SECTION 5.  This Act takes effect January 1, 2028, but only
  if the voters approve the constitutional amendment proposed by the
  90th Legislature, Regular Session, 2027, exempting residence
  homesteads from school district maintenance and operations taxes
  and requiring state replacement of lost revenue.
