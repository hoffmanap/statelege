A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to requirements for commercial and residential property
  insurance policies regarding coverage for a structure other than
  the primary structure covered under the policy.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter A, Chapter 2002, Insurance Code, is
  amended by adding Section 2002.007 to read as follows:
         Sec. 2002.007.  OTHER STRUCTURES COVERAGE. (a) In this
  section:
               (1)  "Other structure" means, with respect to a
  commercial or residential property insurance policy, a structure,
  other than the primary structure, that is located on the land on
  which the primary structure is located but is not attached to the
  primary structure. The term includes a detached structure or other
  appurtenant structure that is located on the land on which the
  primary structure is located but is not otherwise attached to the
  primary structure.
               (2)  "Other structures coverage" means coverage for
  other structures under a commercial or residential property
  insurance policy.
               (3)  "Primary structure" means the principal insured
  building covered under a commercial or residential property
  insurance policy.
         (b)  This section applies only to an insurer writing
  commercial or residential property insurance in this state,
  including a Lloyd's plan, a reciprocal or interinsurance exchange,
  a county mutual insurance company, and a farm mutual insurance
  company.
         (c)  Notwithstanding any other law and subject to Subsection
  (e), a commercial or residential property insurance policy is not
  required to provide other structures coverage.
         (d)  An insurer may issue or renew a commercial or
  residential property insurance policy that provides other
  structures coverage only if:
               (1)  the insured affirmatively elects to include the
  coverage; and
               (2)  the coverage is stated on the declarations page or
  an endorsement form to the policy.
         (e)  A commercial or residential property insurance policy
  that does not provide other structures coverage must contain a
  notice located on the declarations page stating that other
  structures are not covered by the policy.
         (f)  The commissioner may adopt rules necessary to implement
  this section.
         SECTION 2.  The changes in law made by this Act apply only to
  an insurance policy delivered, issued for delivery, or renewed on
  or after January 1, 2028.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
