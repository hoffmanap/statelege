A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
  relating to the production of aggregates near a semiconductor wafer
  manufacturing facility; conforming changes.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Section 382.601(1), Health and Safety Code, is
  amended to read as follows:
               (1)  "Aggregate production operation" means a site from
  which aggregates are being or have been removed or extracted from
  the earth, including the entire areas of extraction, stripped
  areas, haulage ramps, and the land on which the plant processing the
  raw materials is located [has the meaning assigned by Section
  28A.001, Water Code].
         SECTION 2.  Sections 382.651(1) and (3), Health and Safety
  Code, are amended to read as follows:
               (1)  "Aggregate production operation" has the meaning
  assigned by Section 382.601 [28A.001, Water Code].
               (3)  "Portland cement kiln" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 382.601 [means a system, including any solid, gaseous, or
  liquid fuel combustion equipment, used to calcine and fuse raw
  materials, including limestone and clay, to produce portland cement
  clinker].
         SECTION 3.  The change in law made by this Act to Section
  382.601, Health and Safety Code, applies only to a cause of action
  that accrues on or after the effective date of this Act. A cause of
  action that accrues before the effective date of this Act is
  governed by the law applicable to the cause of action immediately
  before the effective date of this Act, and that law is continued in
  effect for that purpose.
         SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
