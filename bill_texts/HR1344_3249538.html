H.R. No. 1344
 
 
 
R E S O L U T I O N
         WHEREAS, Susann Kazunas was named group vice president for
  production engineering and executive engineering officer at Toyota
  Motor North America in May 2025, beginning a new chapter in her
  notable tenure with the company; and
         WHEREAS, Ms. Kazunas began her career in the 1990s at Toyota
  Motor Manufacturing Kentucky, where she primarily worked in the
  stamping and weld shops; she went on to hold a number of leadership
  positions in manufacturing and production engineering, and she
  completed a two-year assignment in Japan before transferring to
  Toyota Motor Manufacturing Texas in San Antonio in 2019; she was
  appointed president of Toyota Texas in February 2024, and her many
  responsibilities in that position included overseeing all
  production and administrative functions at the plant; and
         WHEREAS, In her new role, Ms. Kazunas will relocate to the
  company's engineering headquarters in Georgetown, Kentucky, where
  she will lead equipment preparations and new model deployment for
  all of Toyota's North American manufacturing facilities; she will
  also continue to monitor construction of the new rear axle assembly
  plant at Toyota Texas; and
         WHEREAS, During her time in San Antonio, Ms. Kazunas has
  served on the executive committee for greater:SATX, an organization
  focused on regional economic and workforce development, and she has
  been a member of the executive board of directors for the Greater
  San Antonio Chamber of Commerce; among her many honors, she was
  featured on the 2025 Texas 100 list of impactful leaders compiled by
  The Business Journals media company as well as the South Texas Power
  Players list published in the SanAntonio Business Journal; she was
  also recognized as one of the 100 Leading Women in the North
  American Auto Industry for 2025 by Automotive News; she holds a
  bachelor's degree in organizational management from Midway
  College; and
         WHEREAS, Through her vision, expertise, and commitment to
  excellence, Susann Kazunas has set an inspiring example of
  leadership during her career with Toyota, and she may indeed
  reflect with pride on her accomplishments as she takes on the
  challenges and opportunities of her new position; now, therefore,
  be it
         RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the 89th Texas
  Legislature hereby congratulate Susann Kazunas on her promotion to
  group vice president and executive engineering officer for Toyota
  Motor North America and extend to her sincere best wishes for
  continued success; and, be it further
         RESOLVED, That an official copy of this resolution be
  prepared for Ms. Kazunas as an expression of high regard by the
  Texas House of Representatives.
 
  Lujan
