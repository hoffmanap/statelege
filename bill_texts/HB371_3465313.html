A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the adoption of two budgets by a political subdivision
  that adopts an ad valorem tax rate that exceeds the political
  subdivision's voter-approval tax rate.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 140, Local Government Code, is amended
  by adding Section 140.015 to read as follows:
         Sec. 140.015.  CONTINGENT BUDGETS FOR POLITICAL SUBDIVISION
  ADOPTING AD VALOREM TAX RATE THAT EXCEEDS VOTER-APPROVAL TAX RATE.
  (a) In this section:
               (1)  "Political subdivision" means a county,
  municipality, school district, special purpose district, or other
  subdivision of state government that has jurisdiction limited to a
  geographic portion of the state.
               (2)  "Tax rate" means the ad valorem tax rate of a
  political subdivision for a tax year.
               (3)  "Tax year" has the meaning assigned by Section
  1.04, Tax Code.
               (4)  "Voter-approval election" means an election
  required by law to be automatically held to allow voters to approve
  a political subdivision's tax rate when the political subdivision
  adopts a tax rate that exceeds the political subdivision's
  voter-approval tax rate.
               (5)  "Voter-approval tax rate" means the
  voter-approval tax rate of a political subdivision calculated as
  required for the political subdivision under:
                     (A)  Chapter 26, Tax Code;
                     (B)  Chapter 49, Water Code; or
                     (C)  another provision of law.
         (b)  This section applies only to the fiscal year of a
  political subdivision that begins in a tax year in which the
  political subdivision adopts a tax rate that exceeds the political
  subdivision's voter-approval tax rate.
         (c)  The governing body of a political subdivision shall
  adopt two budgets as provided by Subsections (d) and (e) for a
  fiscal year to which this section applies.
         (d)  The governing body of a political subdivision must adopt
  one budget that assumes the voters approve at the voter-approval
  election the tax rate adopted by the political subdivision. This
  budget is contingent on that voter approval and is the budget for
  the political subdivision if the adopted tax rate is approved by the
  voters at that election.
         (e)  The governing body of a political subdivision must adopt
  a second budget that assumes the voters approve at the
  voter-approval election the voter-approval tax rate. This budget
  is contingent on that voter approval and is the budget for the
  political subdivision if the voter-approval tax rate is approved by
  the voters at that election.
         (f)  A provision of law that applies to the preparation and
  adoption of a budget by a political subdivision applies to both
  budgets required to be adopted under this section, including notice
  and hearing provisions.
