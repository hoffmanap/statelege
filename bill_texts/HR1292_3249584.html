H.R. No. 1292
 
 
 
R E S O L U T I O N
         WHEREAS, Frank Herrera Jr. of San Antonio has demonstrated an
  unwavering commitment to justice and to the economic and political
  empowerment of the Latino and Texas communities; and
         WHEREAS, The founder of the Herrera Law Firm, Mr. Herrera has
  pursued a successful career as a fierce protector of the underdog;
  he has been an innovator in using courtroom technology in trials,
  and he is often consulted by other attorneys for his expertise in
  personal injury, insurance fraud, construction accidents, and
  labor and employment law; benefiting his profession, he has served
  on the board of governors of the Association of Trial Lawyers of
  America and the board of directors of the Texas Trial Lawyers
  Association; and
         WHEREAS, Mr. Herrera is chairman of the board and managing
  partner of Valiente International Ventures, a holding company for
  HERO Assemblers and HERO Logistics; both HERO entities are
  award-winning Tier One Suppliers for Toyota Motor Manufacturing
  Texas, where Tundra and Sequoia models are manufactured, and they
  have been recognized for their commitment to safety and quality in
  handling onsite wheel and tire assembly, logistics, and cross dock
  operations for Team Texas; and
         WHEREAS, Mr. Herrera has given freely of his time and
  resources to numerous civic and charitable organizations and
  educational institutions, including the St. Mary's University
  Alumni Association and San Fernando Historical City Centre
  Foundation; among other leadership roles, he has served on the
  boards of the Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund,
  Central Catholic High School, and the San Antonio Hispanic Chamber
  of Commerce; in 2013, he was named chair of the board of the New
  America Alliance, a group of entrepreneurs and business leaders who
  work to increase access to and control of capital among Latinos and
  to develop the next generation of leaders; he has donated funds to a
  host of community, cultural, and educational organizations as well;
  and
         WHEREAS, In 2024, Mr. Herrera received the Texas Trial
  Lawyers Association Lifetime Achievement Award, and in 2010, he was
  honored with the prestigious Ohtli Award from the government of
  Mexico; his many accolades further include La Prensa's Fourth
  Annual Hispanic Heritage Award for the Legal Profession, the Peter
  Torres Jr. Community Service Award from the Hispanic Issues Section
  of the State Bar of Texas, and MALDEF's Valerie Kantor Award for
  Extraordinary Achievement; and
         WHEREAS, A committed champion of justice and the Latino
  community, Frank Herrera has earned the admiration and appreciation
  of innumerable people through his wide-ranging professional and
  civic contributions; now, therefore, be it
         RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the 89th Texas
  Legislature hereby honor Frank Herrera Jr. for his dedication to
  the advancement of the Latino population in San Antonio and beyond
  and extend to him sincere best wishes for the future; and, be it
  further
         RESOLVED, That an official copy of this resolution be
  prepared for Mr. Herrera as an expression of high regard by the
  Texas House of Representatives.
 
  Martinez Fischer
