A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the maintenance and use of common areas owned by a
  property owners' association.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 209, Property Code, is amended by adding
  Section 209.018 to read as follows:
         Sec. 209.018.  MAINTENANCE AND USE OF ASSOCIATION COMMON
  AREAS. (a) A property owners' association shall maintain and
  regulate the use of all common areas owned by the association,
  including the necessary fixtures and personal property related to
  the common areas, according to any provisions or restrictions in
  the residential subdivision's dedicatory instruments regulating
  the owner's maintenance or use of similar areas, fixtures, and
  personal property related to the owner's residence or lot.
         (b)  If a property owners' association violates this
  section, an owner is entitled to recover from or against the
  property owners' association:
               (1)  injunctive relief;
               (2)  court costs; and
               (3)  reasonable attorney's fees incurred by the owner
  in seeking enforcement of this section.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
