By: Mizani H.B. No. 932
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to a preference in the award of certain municipal and
  county contracts for bidders that document employment eligibility
  verification.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter Z, Chapter 271, Local Government
  Code, is amended by adding Section 271.909 to read as follows:
         Sec. 271.909.  EMPLOYMENT ELIGIBILITY VERIFICATION
  PREFERENCE. (a)  In this section:
               (1)  "Bidder" includes a person submitting a bid or
  proposal in response to a competitive solicitation.
               (2)  "E-verify program" has the meaning assigned by
  Section 673.001, Government Code.
         (b)  This section applies to a contract for goods, services,
  or construction with an estimated value of $100,000 or more for
  which a municipality or county issues a competitive solicitation
  under Chapter 252 or 262 of this code or Chapter 2269, Government
  Code.  This section does not apply to the selection of a provider of
  professional services under Chapter 2254, Government Code, or to
  the extent applying the preference would conflict with federal law
  or a condition of federal funding.
         (c)  A municipality or county shall include in a solicitation
  subject to this section the preference prescribed by Subsection
  (e), the method for applying the preference, and a certification
  form a bidder may submit to qualify for the preference.
         (d)  To qualify for the preference, a bidder must submit with
  its response a certification signed by an authorized representative
  after a reasonable review of the bidder's practices and records.  
  The certification must state that the bidder verifies and will
  continue to verify the employment authorization of employees
  assigned to perform work in the United States under the contract as
  required by 8 U.S.C. Section 1324a and will not knowingly assign a
  person who lacks federal authorization to perform that work. The
  certification must identify one of the following methods:
               (1)  active enrollment and participation in the
  E-verify program for newly hired employees in accordance with
  federal law and program requirements, supported by evidence of the
  bidder's enrollment that does not identify any individual employee;
  or
               (2)  completion and retention of federal employment
  eligibility verification records as required by federal law,
  supported by the bidder's signed attestation that the bidder
  maintains the required records for the employees assigned to
  perform the contract.
         (e)  In awarding a contract subject to this section, a
  municipality or county shall:
               (1)  if the award method permits evaluation of nonprice
  criteria, assign the preference five percent of the total available
  evaluation points or equivalent weight and award those points or
  that weight to a bidder that qualifies under Subsection (d); or
               (2)  if the award method requires award on the basis of
  lowest price, give preference to a bidder that qualifies under
  Subsection (d) only to break a tie between otherwise equally
  eligible lowest bidders, after applying any other preference
  required by law and before using a drawing of lots or another random
  selection method.
         (f)  The municipality or county shall apply the preference
  without reducing the weight assigned to price below any minimum
  required by law. A bidder may compete for and receive a contract
  without electing either method under Subsection (d). This section
  does not alter the requirement to give notice or an opportunity to
  be heard to a lower bidder under other law.
         (g)  If a bidder receives the preference and is awarded the
  contract, the municipality or county shall include the
  certification as a material term of the contract.  The contract must
  require the bidder to obtain a certification using either method
  described by Subsection (d) from each first-tier subcontractor
  supplying on-site labor for the contract before the subcontractor
  begins that work.  A municipality or county may request proof of
  enrollment or a renewed certification during performance to verify
  compliance.  A municipality or county may exercise available
  contract remedies for a materially false certification or material
  noncompliance after providing written notice and an opportunity to
  respond.
         (h)  In administering this section, a municipality or county
  may not require disclosure of an employee's Form I-9, identity
  documents, individual E-verify case information, or other
  personally identifying information from an employment eligibility
  verification record.  A certification under this section does not
  require an employer to prescreen applicants or to verify or
  reverify an employee in a manner inconsistent with federal law or
  E-verify program requirements.
         SECTION 2.  Section 271.909, Local Government Code, as added
  by this Act, applies only to a solicitation first issued on or after
  the effective date of this Act.
         SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
