By: Perez of El Paso H.B. No. 851
 
 
 
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to the repeal of the sales and use tax exemptions for data
  centers and the ineligibility of data centers for enterprise
  project tax refunds.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
  ARTICLE 1.  REPEAL OF DATA CENTER SALES AND USE TAX EXEMPTIONS
         SECTION 1.01.  Sections 151.359 and 151.3595, Tax Code, are
  repealed.
  ARTICLE 2.  CONFORMING AMENDMENTS
         SECTION 2.01.  Section 151.317(a), Tax Code, is amended to
  read as follows:
         (a)  Subject to Section[s] 151.1551[, 151.359, and 151.3595]
  and Subsection (d) of this section, gas and electricity are
  exempted from the taxes imposed by this chapter when sold for:
               (1)  residential use;
               (2)  use in powering equipment exempt under Section
  151.318 or 151.3185 by a person processing tangible personal
  property for sale as tangible personal property, other than
  preparation or storage of prepared food described by Section
  151.314(c-2);
               (3)  use in lighting, cooling, and heating in the
  manufacturing area during the actual manufacturing or processing of
  tangible personal property for sale as tangible personal property,
  other than preparation or storage of prepared food described by
  Section 151.314(c-2);
               (4)  use directly in exploring for, producing, or
  transporting, a material extracted from the earth;
               (5)  use in agriculture, including dairy or poultry
  operations and pumping for farm or ranch irrigation;
               (6)  use directly in electrical processes, such as
  electroplating, electrolysis, and cathodic protection;
               (7)  use directly in the off-wing processing, overhaul,
  or repair of a jet turbine engine or its parts for a certificated or
  licensed carrier of persons or property;
               (8)  use directly in providing, under contracts with or
  on behalf of the United States government or foreign governments,
  defense or national security-related electronics, classified
  intelligence data processing and handling systems, or
  defense-related platform modifications or upgrades;
               [(9)  use directly by a data center or large data center
  project that is certified by the comptroller as a qualifying data
  center under Section 151.359 or a qualifying large data center
  project under Section 151.3595 in the processing, storage, and
  distribution of data;]
               (9) [(10)]  a direct or indirect use, consumption, or
  loss of electricity by an electric utility engaged in the purchase
  of electricity for resale; or
               (10) [(11)]  use in timber operations, including
  pumping for irrigation of timberland.
         SECTION 2.02.  Section 151.317(b), Tax Code, is amended to
  read as follows:
         (b)  The sale, production, distribution, lease, or rental
  of, and the use, storage, or other consumption in this state of, gas
  and electricity sold for the uses listed in Subsection (a), are
  exempted from the taxes imposed by a municipality under Chapter 321
  except as provided by Section[s 151.359(j) and] 321.105.
         SECTION 2.03.  Section 321.208, Tax Code, is amended to read
  as follows:
         Sec. 321.208.  STATE EXEMPTIONS APPLICABLE. The exemptions
  provided by Subchapter H, Chapter 151, apply to the taxes
  authorized by this chapter, except as provided by Section[s
  151.359(j) and] 151.317(b).
  ARTICLE 3.  ENTERPRISE PROJECT TAX REFUNDS
         SECTION 3.01.  Section 151.429, Tax Code, is amended by
  adding Subsection (l) to read as follows:
         (l)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, an
  enterprise project is not eligible for a refund under this section
  of taxes paid on taxable items purchased for use at a qualified
  business site at which a data center is located or is to be located.
  In this subsection:
               (1)  "Data center" means any facility that primarily
  contains electronic equipment used to process, store, or transmit
  digital information.  The term includes a facility used primarily
  for cryptocurrency mining or other distributed ledger or blockchain
  computation and a facility used primarily for the training or
  operation of an artificial intelligence model.
               (2)  "Data center" does not include:
                     (A)  a facility owned and operated by this state,
  a political subdivision of this state, or an institution of higher
  education as defined by Section 61.003, Education Code;
                     (B)  electronic equipment, or the space
  containing electronic equipment, that supports a manufacturing,
  health care, financial, retail, or other business operation
  conducted by the owner or operator of the equipment at the same
  site, if housing that equipment is not the primary use of the site;
  or
                     (C)  a facility used primarily by a
  telecommunications provider to place tangible personal property
  that is used to deliver telecommunications services.
  ARTICLE 4.  ENTERPRISE ZONE PROGRAM
         SECTION 4.01.  Subchapter F, Chapter 2303, Government Code,
  is amended by adding Section 2303.4025 to read as follows:
         Sec. 2303.4025.  DATA CENTER NOT ELIGIBLE. (a) In this
  section:
               (1)  "Data center" means any facility that primarily
  contains electronic equipment used to process, store, or transmit
  digital information.  The term includes a facility used primarily
  for cryptocurrency mining or other distributed ledger or blockchain
  computation and a facility used primarily for the training or
  operation of an artificial intelligence model.
               (2)  "Data center" does not include:
                     (A)  a facility owned and operated by this state,
  a political subdivision of this state, or an institution of higher
  education as defined by Section 61.003, Education Code;
                     (B)  electronic equipment, or the space
  containing electronic equipment, that supports a manufacturing,
  health care, financial, retail, or other business operation
  conducted by the owner or operator of the equipment at the same
  site, if housing that equipment is not the primary use of the site;
  or
                     (C)  a facility used primarily by a
  telecommunications provider to place tangible personal property
  that is used to deliver telecommunications services.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter:
               (1)  a person is not a qualified business under Section
  2303.402 with respect to a trade or business conducted at a data
  center, and the bank or a nominating body may not certify a person
  as a qualified business for that purpose;
               (2)  the bank may not designate as an enterprise
  project a project or activity that consists of or includes the
  construction, expansion, operation, or location of a data center;
               (3)  a governmental body may not provide a benefit,
  incentive, refund, rebate, reduction, waiver, or exemption under
  this chapter to a person with respect to a data center or to a trade
  or business conducted at a data center; and
               (4)  a site at which a data center is located or is to be
  located may not be designated or treated as a qualified business
  site under this chapter.
         (c)  A designation, certification, or agreement made in
  violation of this section is void.
  ARTICLE 5.  TRANSITION; EFFECTIVE DATE
         SECTION 5.01.  (a) The repeal by this Act of Sections
  151.359 and 151.3595, Tax Code, and the changes in law made by this
  Act to Sections 151.317 and 321.208, Tax Code, do not affect tax
  liability accruing before the effective date of this Act.  That
  liability continues in effect as if this Act had not been enacted,
  and the former law is continued in effect for the collection of
  taxes due and for civil and criminal enforcement of the liability
  for those taxes.
         (b)  The change in law made by this Act to Section 151.429,
  Tax Code, applies only to a refund claimed for taxes paid on or
  after the effective date of this Act.  A refund claimed for taxes
  paid before the effective date of this Act is governed by the law in
  effect on the date the taxes were paid, and the former law is
  continued in effect for that purpose.
         (c)  Section 2303.4025, Government Code, as added by this
  Act, applies to a certification made, a designation made, and a
  benefit provided on or after the effective date of this Act,
  including a certification, designation, or benefit under an
  application submitted before that date.
         SECTION 5.02.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
