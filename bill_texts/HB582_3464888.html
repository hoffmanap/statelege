A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to creation of the criminal offense of possessing a
  burning tobacco product or smoking tobacco inside or on the grounds
  of state buildings containing state offices.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Subchapter A, Chapter 2165, Government Code, is
  amended by adding Section 2165.010 to read as follows:
         Sec. 2165.010.  TOBACCO PROHIBITION INSIDE OR ON GROUNDS OF
  STATE BUILDINGS CONTAINING STATE OFFICES; CRIMINAL OFFENSE. (a)
  In this section, "state agency" means a department, commission,
  board, office, or other agency in the executive, legislative, or
  judicial branch of state government created by the state
  constitution or a state statute. The term does not include a
  university system or an institution of higher education as defined
  by Section 61.003, Education Code.
         (b)  A person commits an offense if the person is in
  possession of a burning tobacco product or smokes tobacco inside or
  on the grounds of a state-owned building in which a state agency's
  office is located.
         (c)  An offense under Subsection (b) is a misdemeanor
  punishable by a fine not to exceed $50.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
