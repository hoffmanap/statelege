A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
 
AN ACT
  relating to a prohibition against the required installation or use
  of an intelligent speed assistance device on a motor vehicle.
         BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
         SECTION 1.  Chapter 750, Transportation Code, is amended by
  adding Section 750.004 to read as follows:
         Sec. 750.004.  REQUIRED INSTALLATION OR USE OF INTELLIGENT
  SPEED ASSISTANCE DEVICE PROHIBITED. (a)  In this section,
  "intelligent speed assistance device" means a device that uses
  location-based technology to actively limit a motor vehicle's speed
  to posted or preset speed limits. The term does not include
  technology that is provided by a motor vehicle manufacturer as a
  component of a new motor vehicle and that controls or affects the
  speed of a motor vehicle.
         (b)  Notwithstanding any other law, a governmental entity in
  this state may not require a person to install or use an intelligent
  speed assistance device on a motor vehicle for any reason.
         SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
  a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
  provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
  Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
  Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
